Three ways through the dark

How to steal a quiet moment

Take the orange relic and return to the blue exit; choose Extract to archive it. Two folio leaves in each vault are optional. Nothing advances while inspecting. Every successful move, wait or throw advances every sentry once. Creep moves one tile: silent on normal stone, strength3 on copper. Stride moves up to two tiles: strength5, or7 if it crosses copper. Stealing a relic emits strength7 even while creeping.

Watchers see a straight four-tile beam; listeners see two but hear more faint echoes. Walls stop beams and force sound through corridors. Each normal sound step loses1 strength; entering a curtain loses3. Watchers hear at2+ remaining strength, listeners at0+. Sight beats sound. A heard echo is investigated for three turns including the turn it is heard; a sentry at its target turns to scan. Sentries block one another rather than overlap.

Quiet creeping or waiting on a striped curtain hides you from beams; striding or throwing reveals you. Touching a sentry always captures you, including striding through its old tile. One exposed turn is a warning; a second consecutive exposed turn loses. Breaking sight resets that warning. Sentries see the route you travel, not just its last tile. Recovered chimes return to your two-charge pouch; a sentry reaching a thrown chime confiscates it.

Click/tap a tile to inspect it, then throw or move to that tile if legal; the direction pad moves immediately. The map scrolls internally on narrow screens. With a map tile focused: arrows inspect, WASD move, 1/2 select creep/stride, period waits, T throws at the inspected tile, E extracts, Z undoes. Enter/Space inspect the focused tile. No timer, no reflex requirement. Terrain, patrol memory, loot, chimes, selected tile and pace save automatically. Restart is confirmed; earlier archive records stay safe.

Exact next-action plans include intermediate sight, noisy relic pickup and patrol response; the mint borders apply only if you Wait. Undo restores up to120 session actions, including captures, loot, mastery seals and extracted records. Inspection/pace changes do not fill history. Reload, replan and changing vaults clear history. Four short handmade heists. Best routes prefer more leaves, then fewer exposed turns, more intact chimes, fewer turns and fewer throws.

Each vault has three permanent, separately earnable mastery seals: Ghost for extracting with no exposed turns, Archivist for extracting both leaves, and Bellkeeper for throwing at least one chime and extracting with both intact. During a heist, every card reports whether its goal is ON TRACK, READY or MISSED for this run; permanent earned seals remain checked separately. A single route need not earn all three. Older saves infer earned seals from their best records; older records without chime data can earn Bellkeeper on a future extraction. This is not a content-hours claim.

The midnight collection

Original code-drawn archive, thief, clockwork sentries and relics. No external assets.

Replan this heist?

Current patrols, unbanked loot, warnings and chimes reset. Earlier relics, folio leaves and best routes stay safe.
