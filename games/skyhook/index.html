Skyhook

Small movements. Soft landings.

How to handle a load

Use ← → to move the trolley, ↑ ↓ to change cable length. Buttons and Tab/Space work too. A movement starts the cargo swinging; wait for it to settle. Cross the warehouse with cable at 160 or less. At the striped berth, lower to 200 or more and release when sway is below 18. The landing guide turns green when safe.

Deliver three loads per shift. A roof contact or rough release returns the load to the dock; retry without losing deliveries. Score = trolley/cable moves + 12 per return; lower is better. New shifts vary the berth positions. No timer. Every action and settled motion saves.
