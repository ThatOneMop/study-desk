THE SALVAGE NOTEBOOK

Bell Diver

Bring three lost instruments back to the diving bell. No clock: oxygen changes only when you swim.

At the bell

Your first dive

Follow the rails of light into the wreck. Gold discs are salvage; the diamond is an instrument. Swim onto one, then collect. Return to the gold bell at the top to bank everything.

Your bag holds 4 weight. At 3 or 4 weight, swimming upward costs 2 air instead of 1. Fins remove that extra cost. Air pockets restore 8 air once per dive. All pockets refill at the bell. The bell restores your tank for free.

Running out of air returns you safely but drops all unbanked cargo back into the wreck. Banked finds stay safe. Tank upgrades add 6 air each. Next wreck unlocks after banking its instrument.

Tap adjacent water or use the buttons. Focus the map for arrow keys, Space to collect and B to breathe. Pause before leaving; progress saves on this browser.

Original code-drawn underwater art. Three compact authored wrecks.
