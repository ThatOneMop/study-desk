One leaf. One hand. Five thousand places to discover.

Click a leaf to pick it. Hold & drag to blow leaves off the lawn, or sweep them into the basket for double acorns. Chain a sweep for a combo. Upgrade your hands, get gloves, rakes, grabbers and vacuums. Clear 85% of an area, then pay to open the next gate.

5,000 generated areas · 20,000 upgrade ranks · Autosave

How to play

Rake it in.

Click or tap a leaf to pick it. Tougher leaves take a few tugs.
Hold & drag to blow nearby leaves. Leaves blown off the lawn pay acorns; leaves swept into the leaf basket pay double. Quick sweeps build a combo.
Upgrades: strength picks tougher leaves and blows heavier ones, reach widens your gust, tools pick several at once, appraisal raises every leaf.
Areas: clear 85% of an area and pay the gate to open the next. Golden leaves are worth ×5.
Keyboard: arrows select · Space / Enter pick · Esc closes menus.

A little help for the garden

Little by little.

Four upgrade tracks, 5,000 ranks each. One new rank per track unlocks in every area. Grabber at equipment rank 20 enables hold-to-pick.
