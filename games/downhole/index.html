Downhole

An original digging-and-selling mine. Dig deep, haul ore, upgrade, go deeper.

Original game · Three.js (MIT) · sounds synthesised in-browser · no external assets

How to play

  • WASD move · mouse look (click to lock) · Space jetpack once bought.
  • Left click / hold: dig the block you aim at. Harder rock needs a better pickaxe.
  • Deeper blocks hide better ore: copper → silver → gold → void crystal.
  • E on the surface: sell ore and buy upgrades at the shack.
  • Falls over 3 blocks hurt. E at depth shows your cargo. Death drops unsold cargo.
  • Esc menu · progress saves in this browser.
