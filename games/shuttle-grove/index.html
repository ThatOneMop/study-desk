PLACE. RECOVER. RETURN.

Shuttle Grove

A quiet court, a thoughtful rally.

Aim across the net

First to 3 points. Stretch your opponent, vary the scoring square, and budget stamina for your next return. No real-time timer.

Read the court

The blue player travels to the incoming shuttle automatically. Each two steps cost one stamina (rounded up). Clears recover three stamina; drops cost one and smashes cost three. Maximum player stamina is six. Choose Recover centre to move one square centrally after returning, paying one extra stamina when needed; Hold position saves that stamina. Win without conceding to earn a persistent clean-match badge.

A clear gives the receiver five steps, a drop three and a smash two. Aim far from the opponent to stretch them, but preserve enough stamina to reach their return. Opponents pay one stamina per step and recover toward the centre after clears/drops, spending one stamina. Fern has six stamina and rests one per rally; Rowan has seven but no passive rest; Ash has five and rests one. Their shot cycle shifts after each point. They also anticipate the last square you scored on, gaining one reach against it. The forecast tells you if you will miss or win immediately.

Touch or Tab and Space/Enter operate all controls. Choices save automatically. Restart keeps your best match records.
