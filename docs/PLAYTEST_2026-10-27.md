# Gummy Bay playtest and feel pass, 27 Oct 2026 (v144)

Played between about 03:40 and 04:20 AEDT (UTC+11). The before build was v142 plus a read-only QA hook. The after build is this branch.

## Method

- **Integrated playtest** (`playtest.cjs`): headless Chrome with real CDP keyboard and touch input on Low, on Balanced and on a 390x844 touch phone. Each run goes through the menu, the story start, free roam, walk/sprint/jump, a kerb, wall sliding, the skateboard, the van, mixing, a deal with NPC effect bubbles, the phone opened over the effects, a police chase, the story trying to fire during the chase, the arcade, evening, midnight and the morning of day 2.
  - Every step audits the HUD for overlapping or clipped elements and takes a screenshot (`/workspace/overnight/shots/feel-{before,after}-<mode>-NN-<step>.png`).
- **Feel metrics** (`feelsim.cjs` with the `window.__feel.sim` hook): headless SwiftShader renders at only 2–5 fps, so feel is measured by stepping the real `update()` at a fixed 60 Hz with scripted keys. The hook doesn't change gameplay.
- **Reference:** Schedule One's first-person feel. Walking responds fast, sprint builds up a bit, the camera stays still, the head-bob is mild, wall sliding never snags, kerbs step up smoothly, and vehicles take a moment to reach full steering lock. No Schedule One assets or code were used.

## Interaction prompts

- The prompt stays at 60–62% height, which is clear of the crosshair, the HUD and the new touch buttons. On phones, tapping the prompt or pressing **USE** does the same as E.
- The "Exit vehicle [F]" chip is tappable, and it says "Stop to exit" while moving.
- The objective card has a stronger text shadow, so it stays readable over bright sky.
