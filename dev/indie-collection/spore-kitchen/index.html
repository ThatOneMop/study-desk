How to run your kitchen

No clock is ticking. Pick a seed, then an empty bed to plant. Garden actions grow all mushrooms. Tap an unripe bed to mist it, or a ripe one to harvest. Choose two basket ingredients, a cooking method and a guest order. Match Earth / Glow / Heat to earn three stars. Serve all three orders with at least 7 of 9 stars to open the next service. Seeds are free; nothing rots.

Keyboard: Tab to a control, Enter or Space to use it. W waits. R opens retry confirmation. Progress saves after each action.

One step, this visit only. Also reverses earnings.
