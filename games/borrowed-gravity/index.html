OPERATOR’S NOTES

Borrow the pull.
Mind what it leaves behind.

Move with A/D or left/right when gravity points up or down. When a wall becomes your floor, use W/S or up/down to move along it. Space jumps away from the surface beneath your feet. A short jump buffer and edge grace help early or slightly late presses.

Choose a source in the assignment panel (Q cycles sources). Get within range with a clear line, then press E or Borrow source. The source’s arrow becomes your gravity, and its linked barrier opens. You can only carry one source at a time.

R returns your borrowed weight from anywhere. Swapping to a second source automatically restores the first. A transfer that would close a barrier around you is blocked until you move clear.

The drift preview shows 1.5 seconds with no directional input, under your current pull or after a proposed transfer. It does not move the player or change the save. A red path warns of a scanner; you can still steer away.

Recover all three glowing samples, return every borrowed weight, and reach the green exit. A scanner collision loses only the current attempt; the archive keeps completed rooms. Restart lets you try again.

The first gallery unlocks the steady harness (hold Shift): slower movement and falling. The second extends borrowing reach from 155 to 205. Neither upgrade requires grinding.

Touch: hold the large movement buttons. Two fingers can combine movement and jumping. Tap Borrow or Return to transfer gravity. Losing focus pauses and releases controls. Reload restores the saved room paused.
