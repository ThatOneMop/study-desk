A reef is a neighbourhood.

Place up to eight habitat pieces. Meet all three goals, then complete your survey to unlock the next coast.

  • Coral: your largest side-connected coral colony counts. Diagonals do not connect.
  • Nurseries: each shell needs coral directly beside it, plus open water beside it connected to an edge. Shells on the edge already reach the sea.
  • Kelp: only side-connected groups of three or more count as forest.

Water flows only through empty squares. Stones and every habitat block it. Keep channels open!

Earn extra medals at the vitality targets. Replay any unlocked reef; your best survey is kept. Undo is free.

Keyboard: 1–3 choose, R rotates, arrow keys move on the grid, Enter previews / plants, Z undoes. Mouse or touch: tap a square to preview, then Plant habitat. The preview reports exact changes, including lost nurseries. Show sea channels to see which empty squares reach the edge.
