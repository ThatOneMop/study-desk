Your first casting

Weigh exactly four ingots using the requested Emberite/Mooniron recipe. Bellows adds 3 glow and costs one fuel; vent removes 1 glow. Melt needs at least 6 glow. These are fictional game units, not real metalworking instructions.

Select a section of the mould. Fine details hold 1 unit at 4–6 glow; body sections hold 2 at 6–9; heavy cores hold 3 at 8–11. Trickle pours 1, stream pours 2. A pour checks its starting glow, then spends one glow per unit. Complete sections in the owner’s requested order; numbered badges record that order, and undo can recover a missed mark. Two rough pours spoil a casting. A cold, empty-fuel crucible also loses.

Open the mould when every section is filled. Earn a seal for delivery, one for a clean casting that honours the owner’s mark, and one for fuel efficiency. Replay orders for better seals. Arrow keys move among sections; Enter/Space selects. Z undoes up to 100 craft actions in this session, including spoiled castings and earned seals. Nothing advances on a timer. Autosave follows each craft action.
