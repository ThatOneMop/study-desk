      How the pact works

      Each neighbour holds six cards. All hands and all six wind suits are visible. The leader plays first, then play goes clockwise: You → Wren → Moss → You. Follow the led suit if you have it; otherwise choose any card. Every card has a suit and a rank from 1 to 6.

      The highest wind-suit card wins. If nobody plays the wind suit, the highest card in the led suit wins. The winner takes one harvest of the led suit, then leads the next trick. A Berry 1 can beat a Leaf 6 when Berry is the wind—but if Leaf was led, the harvest is still a Leaf.

      Wren and Moss follow a fixed, visible rule: when leading, play their lowest rank, breaking ties Berry, Nut, Leaf. When following, play their weakest legal card that beats the current winner; if none can win, play their weakest legal card. Strength is wind suit, then led suit, then other suits, with higher ranks stronger. They do not think ahead. Select your card to preview the complete trick before committing.

      Your baskets must match the promise exactly at the end. You may make one woodland offer before playing: it spends your offer, and any harvest you win on that trick is left behind. With the offer checked, you may invite either neighbour to lead the next trick instead of keeping the lead. Your won harvest still stays in the woodland. If you lose, the invitation has no effect and the offer is spent. The final trick has no invitation because there is no next lead. If a basket overflows, undo or keep playing to learn the remaining hand. There is no clock. Undo and restart preserve completed-picnic records.

      Tab, Enter and Space operate controls. From the page or one of your cards: 1–6 selects a card by its current position, Enter plays it, G toggles the woodland offer, Z undoes. Other controls keep their normal keyboard behaviour. Pause stops input.
