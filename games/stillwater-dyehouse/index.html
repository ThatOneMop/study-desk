Stillwater Dyehouse

Every drop carries a little colour.

Add a cup of pigment

Transfer the selected blend

At the dye bench

Choose a pot, then add single cups of Rose, Indigo or Marigold. Each order needs two cups in the exact displayed proportions. Extra dye stays in the pot after delivery.

Transfers carry the current mixture, never separate pigments. Your measure pours up to one or two cups, limited by the source and space in the receiving pot. The pots hold 4, 3 and 2 cups. A rinse discards all dye in the selected pot; you have only three spare cups across the three orders.

No timer. Touch or Tab and Space/Enter operate every control. Every action saves. Retry keeps records.
