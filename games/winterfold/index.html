Field notes

Drag a ribbon downhill. Its colour follows the steepness: orange trail, then green, blue, red, and black. Runs are one-way, the way you dragged.

Guests are little shapes. A triangle is looking for a summit, a diamond for a viewpoint, and so on. They only ski runs up to their own colour. Green guests cannot take a black pitch. Lifts carry everyone uphill.

They must be back at a station by 18:00. If anyone is still out, the day is over. Each morning you pick one refill: lifts, ribbon, upgrades, cannons, or tree cuts. New stops flash until a line ends near them.

Surface lifts need a clear path. Chairs cross trees. Cabins can cross a cliff. Cannons lay snow on bare ground. Cut trees one clump at a time. Upgrade a busy lift, a groomed run, or a cannon.

Keys: 1–5 tools, M move, D draw, X erase, Q and E change the clock, Space pauses, Ctrl+Z undoes a build. Sandbox turns the stocks and the dusk rule off.
