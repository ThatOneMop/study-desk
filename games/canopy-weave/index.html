ROOM FOR EVERY VINE

Canopy Weave

Untangle the trellis. Move leaf knots until every vine has its own clear path.

A gardener’s guide

Drag a lettered leaf knot to another dot, or select its letter button and tap an empty dot. The arrow buttons or keyboard arrow keys move the selected knot one dot at a time. Keyboard arrows work when focus is on the trellis or page; other controls keep their normal keys.

Dashed rust-coloured vines are tangled. Vines may meet at a shared leaf, but must not cross or overlap anywhere else. A vine also cannot pass through an unrelated leaf. All knots stay on separate dots. A move may temporarily add tangles: making room can help.

Compact garden: unlocked after completing a trellis. Every vine must fit within a straight-line reach of four grid steps, as well as having no tangles. A gold reach circle surrounds the selected leaf; its connected leaves must end inside or on that circle. Dotted gold vines are too long, and their letters are listed above. Temporary stretching is allowed while arranging. Compact records are separate. Changing mode restarts the layout and keeps both records.

During dragging, the preview is temporary; release to commit one move. Cancelling a drag or pausing leaves the saved layout unchanged. Clear every tangle to unlock the next trellis. Undo is unlimited; retry keeps records. Replay for fewer moves. There is no timer and no penalty for experimenting.
