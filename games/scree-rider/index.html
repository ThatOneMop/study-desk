KEEP YOUR LINE

Scree Rider

A quiet trail. A little momentum.

Lean into the line

Set your pace

Read the trail

There is no real-time timer. Choose lean and pace, check the exact forecast, then ride one section. Choices stay selected until changed. Left/right changes sideways drift by one; Hold lean keeps the current drift, it does not centre the bike.

Pedal raises speed, brake lowers it. Speed ranges from 1 to 4. Faster bends push you farther toward the outside edge. Stay between −4 and +4. Gaps need speed 3 or 4; roots need speed 1 or 2.

A scrape costs one of three bike-condition points and resets your line and speed. Finish with condition remaining, then improve your simulated descent time. Touch or Tab and Space/Enter operate all controls. Every choice saves; restart keeps records.
