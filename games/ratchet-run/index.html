RIDER’S HANDBOOK

Easy on the cargo.

The dotted arc previews a hop with your current controls. On later attempts, the pale ghost replays your best delivery so you can compare lines.

Hold ↑ / W to accelerate and ↓ / S to brake. Use ← / A and → / D for small balance adjustments. Press Space to hop before a gap or over cobbles.

On touch, hold the large riding buttons. You can hold throttle and tap hop together. Release a control to stop using it.

Brake in the striped bay to deliver. Blue parcels add a seal; all three cargo straps add another. Deliveries unlock the next route. Your best seals and times stay saved.

The game pauses when focus leaves. Your current ride resumes paused after a reload. Restart keeps your best deliveries.
