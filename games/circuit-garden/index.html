Build a living microgrid

Choose a module and a roof tile. Adjacent modules, cables and garden beds connect on all four sides; no rotation is needed. Rock is blocked. Replacing/removing a module refunds its cost. Every bed must connect to solar or wind before commissioning.

Each weather card gives solar/wind output. A shaded solar tile produces one less, and sheltered wind produces one less (never below zero). Each battery stores 4 units, can release at most 2 per interval and charges at most 2 from surplus. Separate circuits cannot share power.

After commissioning, switch on only the loads you want to serve, choose first priority and run an interval. Fully powered beds grow one stage. An unserved switched-on bed browns out; three brownouts trip the grid. Off beds safely wait; mature beds switch off automatically. Mature every bed before the forecast ends.
