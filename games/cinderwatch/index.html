THE RIDGE WATCH

Cinderwatch

Keep the cabins standing. Give the forest a tomorrow.

Taking the watch

Select a tile, then cut unburned brush or douse a fire. Each intervention advances one turn. Refill your two-charge water pack when needed, but the fire moves while you refill.

Every burning tile ignites its downwind neighbour after your action. Fire burns out after three turns. Rocks, wet ground, ash and cut breaks stop it. Read the six-turn repeating wind forecast. Protect every cabin until all flames are gone.

Win to open the next clearing. Your record is the most brush left intact, so excessive clearing has a cost. Retry keeps your records. Tab and Space work on tiles and tools. Nothing advances while you are away.

A fictional tactical puzzle with original artwork, not real firefighting guidance.
