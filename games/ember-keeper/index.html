Keep watch in a small walled orchard. Every step gives the cinders a turn.

The keeper’s handbook

Move with arrows or tap an adjacent square. Step toward a cinder to strike instead of moving. Small cinders take one hit; crowned cinders take two.

After every action, cinders attack you if adjacent; otherwise they attack the beacon if adjacent, or move toward it. Enemy marks show their remaining health. There is no timer.

F spends 3 sparks to deal 1 damage to every enemy within two steps. Each defeated cinder gives 1 spark. R spends 2 sparks to restore 4 beacon strength while beside it.

Space guards against all strikes aimed at you this turn, but the beacon still needs defending. Each cleared wave restores 3 health and gives 3 sparks before the next wave. Survive four waves for dawn.

Z undoes any action in the current wave. Your active night and best completed watch save on this browser. Restart begins a new night.
