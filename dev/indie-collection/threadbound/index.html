How to weave a crossing

You are the small ivory moth with a spool. Pale horizontal branches are solid; deep roots are scenery. Click a gold pin or its numbered button to hook it within 345 units. You can transfer directly to another reachable pin. A rope only pulls: swing it with left/right, reel shorter with up, or pay out with down. Release keeps momentum. The dotted mint/coral path forecasts a branch landing/safety return for 2.5 seconds with your current pump direction held; it is not an autopilot. The displayed distance must be at most 345 to hook. Jump from a branch with J.

Keyboard: focus the canopy after starting. Arrows or A/D/W/S move/reel; 1–4 hook a pin, Q selects the next, X hooks the selection, C hooks the nearest new reachable pin (preferring east), Space releases, E kindles the lantern, Escape pauses. Touch: hold pump/reel buttons for continuous control; quick taps give one 0.1-second nudge. Tap pin, jump or release. Clicking a visible pin in the canopy also hooks it.

Land on the striped middle branch to knot a checkpoint. A fall returns you there with collected silver seeds. No lives or countdown. Reach the far lantern and choose Kindle. Earn one stitch for arrival, another for all seeds, another for no falls. Four short handmade crossings; no content-hours claim. Exact motion and best stitches autosave; reload starts paused.

Original code-drawn moth, forest and thread. No external assets. Development-only; not a finished release.
