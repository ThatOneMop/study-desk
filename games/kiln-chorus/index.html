    Firing notebook
      How the clay sings

      Each action advances the entire kiln once. Stoke spends one fuel and raises chamber heat by 3 (maximum 12). Bank lowers it by 1; Vent lowers it by 3. Soak fire spends one fuel to keep the chamber at its current heat for one action. Clay still moves toward that heat and earns glaze normally: holding the chamber does not freeze the pieces or prevent overheating. It is unavailable when the chamber is cold or fuel is gone. Heat never falls below zero.

      After the chamber changes, every piece still inside moves toward that temperature by its warmth response. Thick clay responds slowly. A piece earns one glaze mark each turn its own warmth is inside its marked firing band. Marks stay when it cools.

      Once all its marks are filled, draw the piece onto the cooling shelf. Drawing happens before the other pieces warm or cool, and lowers chamber heat by 1. A ready piece left in the kiln can still overheat. Any piece above its upper limit spoils the load. Draw every piece to finish; save fuel for a better record.

      One-use baffle: select Protect on a piece to hold its warmth unchanged for the next action. It still earns a glaze mark if already in its firing band. Other pieces respond normally. This can buy time for slow clay without overheating fast clay. You cannot protect the piece you are drawing. Tap Protect again to cancel; selecting is only a preview. The baffle is spent when the action is performed, even if the load fails; undo restores it.

      Every action has an exact forecast. Undo and retry are unlimited; records stay. Keyboard 1/2/3/4 selects Stoke/Bank/Vent/Soak fire; A/B/C selects a ready piece to draw; Enter performs the selected action when focus is on the page. Buttons retain native keyboard behavior. All controls also work by tapping. No timing challenge.
