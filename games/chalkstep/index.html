FEET FIRST · REACH WITH CARE

Chalkstep

A quiet climb, one hold at a time.

How to climb

Select a hand or foot, then a bright hold. Each limb can travel up to three grid steps (horizontal plus vertical). Keep your body within three rows and three columns; hands must stay at least as high as your feet.

Hand moves use one grip. Foot moves use none. Rest when both feet share a height and both hands are no more than two rows above them. Each ledge restores grip only once per climb.

Finish with both hands on the highest holds and both feet at height four or higher. There is no timer. If you run out of grip, reposition your feet to rest or retry. Records survive retries. Buttons support touch, Tab, Space and Enter; every move saves.
