How the post gets there

A / D or ← / → move. Space / W / ↑ jump. Shift / X dash once, then land to recharge. E delivers at the yellow postbox. Esc pauses. Touch: hold an arrow. Tap Jump for a short hop, hold it to jump higher. Tap Dash to cross a gap.

Deliver the letter for one medal. Collect all three blue parcels for another; finish without falling for a third. Mid-route flags protect your progress. Eight falls end a route. Coral steam is dangerous while lit. Dark vents are safe.

Progress autosaves during play. Reload always pauses before resuming. Route times are personal records, never a countdown. Original canvas art; no third-party assets.
