Lockwater

Carry the cargo uphill. Bring your boat home.

Upper chamber

Your first delivery

1. Fill both chambers to the middle gate’s sill height. Your boat floats up and down with its chamber.

2. Sail only between equal water levels. Transfer moves one unit from the higher chamber to the lower without spending reservoir water. Drain throws a unit away.

3. Reach the hill dock to unload, then return to the sea. Save water first, then improve your turn record. If too much water is drained before delivery, restart.

Keyboard: focus the canal and use ←/→ to sail. Tab to water controls and use Space or Enter. There is no clock.
