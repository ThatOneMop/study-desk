## Candidate90
Original deliberate four-limb climbing puzzle. Select left/right hand or foot, move to a reachable unoccupied hold, keep the body within its span and hands above feet, and top out with high foot support. Hands consume grip; feet move without spending grip. Reposition both feet onto one height and keep hands nearby to rest, with each ledge usable once. Three authored walls, best-move records, touch/native keyboard controls, scoped saves and explicit corrupt-save recovery are implemented. No timer or fall countdown.

This adds articulated limb placement and grip/rest sequencing to the slate. It is distinct from crane pendulum handling, cycling momentum and badminton court movement. Original SVG climber/wall artwork and code are MIT; no external assets or imported collection.
