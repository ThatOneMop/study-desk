Ink stays on paper

Match the commission exactly within five prints. Sea, rose and sun inks combine wherever they overlap. Ink cannot be erased, but Undo always lets you try again.

Choose a stencil and ink, rotate it, then tap a bed square or use arrow keys to position it. The outlined cells show where the ink will land. Press Print or Space to stamp.

Use “show only the selected ink” to understand a colour layer. A stencil spilling off the paper cannot print. Win to unlock the next commission; replay to use fewer prints. There is no timer.
