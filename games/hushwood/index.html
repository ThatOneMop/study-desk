A LANTERN BORROWED FROM THE NIGHT

Hushwood

Leave only soft footsteps.

● You · ◆ Lantern · ⌂ Home · Arrows: guards
Amber: watched now · Dotted teal: watched after your next step

Distract with a pebble

Into the grove

Reach the seed lantern at the top right, then return to your home at the bottom left. Hedges block both walking and sight. Guards walk after every step, wait or throw. Standing on a guard or in its new sightline means you are spotted.

Read the dotted next-turn sightlines before moving. A guard sees three squares straight ahead, stopping at a hedge. A pebble travels up to two squares and turns the nearer guard toward its landing place for two turns. Throwing also advances the patrols. You have two pebbles. After pickup, the lantern’s glow also reveals every adjacent square around each guard. A distracted guard ignores that glow and looks only toward the pebble. Save pebbles for a quicker return, or take a longer safe route. Earn a permanent glow-return seal for each completed watch. Older in-progress saves keep their original rules until retry; historical turn records remain.

Retry preserves records. New watch changes the starting patrol timing, not the grove layout. Tab and Space operate every control; touch works directly. There is no running clock, and every action saves. This is a compact stealth prototype.
