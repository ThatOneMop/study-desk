MISSION BRIEF

Your ride leaves with you.

Recover the three glowing cores, then hold the landing zone for 12 seconds. Your shield restores after avoiding damage. Move constantly and save your dodge for trouble.

WASD move · Mouse aim · Hold click / J fire · Space dodge · E recover

Read the field

Teal is your landing zone. Yellow rings mark the cores. Stand nearby and hold E / Recover for two seconds. Once all three are yours, return to the landing zone and stay inside its ring until pickup arrives. Leaving pauses the extraction clock.

Keep your suit together

Hold J or the touch Fire button to aim at the nearest threat. Mouse aiming takes over when you click. Space dodges with brief protection; its ring recharges in three seconds. Clear threats to earn supply drops. Press 1 / 2 / 3 to select unlocked weapons, or tap the armory buttons below.
