CARRY THE LIGHT

Before you strike a match

Move with WASD or the touch arrows. Mouse movement aims; click the field or press Space for one flash. Arrow keys aim in four directions. Quick keyboard and touch taps queue one simulation step; holding an attack never repeats it. Release and press again once recharged. Aim assist tracks the nearest visible shadow; switch it off for manual aim.

A flash costs 8 fuel, damages every shadow in its cone and briefly stuns survivors. Rocks block the beam. The current-cone note names what this snapshot would hit, not where moving shadows will be later. Motes and glass-wings need one flash, briar shades two, stonebacks three. Gather each fallen ember to recover 7 fuel.

Shadows show a warning line before lunging. Move aside or Shift to dodge (4 fuel). Five hits end the attempt. At an oil brazier, press E or Take oil for 24 fuel; each has two charges. Running out of oil with no loose ember or brazier charges ends the attempt explicitly.

Collect every ember and reach the east gate. One medal for escape, one for taking no hits, one for staying within the clearing's refill target. Replays award only better records. Spend medals between attempts on a wider lens or fuel lining.

Escape pauses. Losing focus or switching away pauses without auto-resuming. Saves retain the exact clearing; reload starts paused. Retry resets only this attempt, preserving medals/upgrades. Short original prototype, no duration claim.
