Shuttle Grove

A quiet court, a thoughtful rally.

Aim across the net

First to 3 points. Choose a square and shot, read the forecast, then return. No real-time timer.

Read the court

The blue player travels to the incoming shuttle automatically. Each two steps cost one stamina (rounded up). Clears recover three stamina; drops cost one and smashes cost three. Maximum stamina is six.

A clear gives the receiver five steps, a drop three and a smash two. Aim far from the opponent to stretch them, but preserve enough stamina to reach their return. The opponent recovers two stamina per rally, then pays their shot cost. Opponents cycle clear/drop/smash in different orders. The forecast tells you if you will miss or win immediately.

Touch or Tab and Space/Enter operate all controls. Choices save automatically. Restart keeps your best match records.
