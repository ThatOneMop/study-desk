THE CLOCKWORK COURTS

Brasswake

Borrow their fire. Turn it back.

How to turn the tide

Every action advances the court by one turn. Red bolts move one tile along their arrow. If a bolt reaches you while you guard, it becomes blue and flies in your chosen direction. Blue bolts damage sentries; you have no other attack.

Each reflection spends one shield charge. Walking restores one, up to two. Guarding an empty tile wastes no charge. Without a charge, a hit costs one heart. Three hits end the attempt.

Sentries fire every third turn when you share an unobstructed row or column. Sentries marked “2 hits” need two reflections. Red outlines show where hostile bolts will land next. You can move away, wait, or redirect them. Clear a court to unlock the next; saved best turn counts reward replay.

Keyboard: arrows walk; I/J/K/L guard up/left/down/right; Space waits. Focus the court first. On phones, use the fixed action panel or tap adjacent floor to walk.

Original vector artwork and combat rules. Three compact authored courts.
