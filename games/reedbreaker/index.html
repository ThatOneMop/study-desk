Field guide

You are the green ranger; numbered copper beetles are your opponents. Water is blue and rocks are grey. Walk one tile; dash up to three, stopping at the first beetle. A dash shoves it one tile and skips its response. Water removes a beetle; rocks or another beetle block the shove. The ranger never enters water.

The reed hook costs one energy. Choose the direction toward an adjacent beetle: you step backward onto free land and pull it into your old square. The hooked beetle skips its response, but the others still act. Water, rock or another beetle behind you blocks the hook for free. Use it to draw a beetle away from an obstacle; it leaves less energy for a dash.

Vault: spend one energy to jump over an adjacent beetle onto the land immediately behind it. The vaulted beetle stays put and skips its response; all others still act. Water, rocks or another beetle at the landing block the move for free. Use it to change approach sides without shoving the beetle.

Brace: spend one energy to stay in place facing the chosen direction. An adjacent beetle attacking from that direction is blocked and pushed one tile away; water removes it. Rock or another beetle prevents the push, but the frontal hit is still blocked. Side and rear attacks still deal damage. Beetles farther away approach normally and do not attack on that same turn. Bracing at empty space still spends energy, so inspect the forecast first. Walk to recover energy.

After your move, each remaining beetle attacks for 1 if adjacent, otherwise moves one tile closer. Beetle 1 tries horizontal first; beetle 2 tries vertical first. They never move into water. The preview includes all responses, so use it to judge danger. Clear a bank to unlock the next. Retry keeps records; there is no timer.

Plan before moving: enable the checkbox to make direction taps or arrow keys select a read-only plan. The second board shows the exact ranger and beetle positions after all responses. Perform planned turn commits it; Cancel plan costs nothing. Enter also commits when focus is on the page. Changing mode, pausing, opening a dialog or hiding the game cancels the plan. Plans are not saved. Turn this option off for the original immediate controls.

Undo turn (U): rewind a committed turn, including every beetle response, shield and energy cost. You can recover from defeat or test another approach after a win. Earned records stay. Undo never crosses into the previous bank. The response notes name each attacking, countered, displaced or approaching beetle.

Arrow keys move. D chooses dash; W chooses walk; H chooses hook; B chooses brace; V chooses vault. Buttons support touch. A dash needs both energy points; walking restores one. Blocked actions cost nothing. Progress saves automatically.
