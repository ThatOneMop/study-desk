WELCOME TO THE WORKSHOP

Build the sound in motion.

Match the requested score by routing a small supply of reusable brass beads. Every turn of the crank moves each bead one track position. A bead at an open exit strikes its instrument and returns to your supply. Then the selected new bead enters its track.

Travel times: a bead fed now sounds two more cranks later on the short track, three on the middle track, four on the long track. One bead can be fed each beat. A bead that sounds this beat can be recycled into that beat's feed.

Later commissions unlock exit brakes and a rotating selector. Brakes hold the end bead, allowing the track to fill behind it. If the inlet is occupied or the supply is empty, the proposed turn is rejected without spending a beat. The selector changes all three exit instruments, including beads already travelling.

Silent catches: twice per take, select an exiting bead to recycle without a note. Its brake must be open and the bead already at the exit. A selected catch applies to one crank only. It can avoid an extra note or recycle a bead for another track, but it can also miss a required note. Preview before cranking; toggle the catcher again to cancel. Each catch counts like one brake beat for the third seal.

Score: collect at least 75% accuracy to complete a commission. Each missing or extra note is one error; beads still in the tracks at the end also count as extras. A perfect score earns two seals, plus a third for staying within the brake-beat target. One beat with any brakes held counts once. Best records survive rewinds and restarts.

Read the pipeline: the preview names the exact notes, missing/extra notes, remaining supply and any blocked inlet before you crank. The three-beat strip shows the current proposal, then two more cranks with no new beads and unchanged brakes/selector. Changing a control updates the preview without spending a beat. On the last beat, it includes any undelivered beads and the final result.

Keys: 1/2/3 select a feed track; 0 skips feeding; A/S/D toggle brakes; E rotates exits; 4/5/6 select a one-turn silent catcher; Space cranks; Z rewinds. Repeat keys and shortcuts while a dialog is open are ignored. Enter activates a focused button. Tap controls work too. P belongs to Study Desk.

Sound is optional, starts only when enabled and uses original synthesized tones. Every musical event also has a visual mark; headphones are not required.
