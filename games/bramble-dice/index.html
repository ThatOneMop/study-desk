A small journey, at your pace.

Four dice roll at the start of each turn. Pin any to keep, then reroll the rest up to twice. Changing jobs or pins costs nothing. The same seed gives the same dice sequence.

Assign all four dice: attack adds their points, then subtracts the enemy's armour once; guard subtracts from incoming damage; repair pools points and heals 1 per 3, up to 18 health. Repair happens before incoming damage.

Reserve one die: choose Reserve as its job. It gives no attack, guard, repair or gear bonus this turn, but starts the next turn in the same slot with that exact face. This also survives an encounter clearing. The other three dice roll normally. You may reroll or turn the carried die afterward; reserve does not pin it. Selecting a different reserve clears the old reserve job, so reassign that die before resolving. The next roll still consumes its normal random sequence, keeping seeds consistent.

Defeating a creature cancels its attack. Thorns still hurt when you use attack dice, even on a finishing blow, and bypass guard. The preview shows exact outcomes.

After the first two creatures choose equipment, recover 4 health, and continue. Equipment stacks but lasts only for that trail. The Three-leaf clasp rewards using all three jobs in the same turn. The Acorn sling makes low attack dice valuable: each 1 or 2 adds three extra attack per sling. Consider these before rerolling small numbers; the forecast includes the conditional bonus. Reach home past the owl to record your fewest turns. There is no clock. Retry preserves your record and repeats the seed; New dice seed starts the next sequence.

Use Compare this plan to set aside a complete allocation. Try other jobs and compare health and reserved dice side by side. Swap with comparison exchanges two complete plans, or restores the comparison if the trial is incomplete. Rerolling, turning over dice, resolving, restarting or reloading clears the temporary comparison. The following enemy move is shown to help decide what to reserve.

All decisions save automatically. Native buttons and selects work with keyboard or touch. On a focused die card, 1 assigns attack, 2 guard, 3 repair and 4 reserve; K pins that die. No undo after rolling or resolving.

Turn over a die to its opposite face (1↔6, 2↔5, 3↔4) for one reroll. Other dice, jobs and keeps stay. Kept dice can be turned deliberately. Each turn still has only two rerolls shared between rolling and turning. Focus a die card and press F, or use its Turn over button.
