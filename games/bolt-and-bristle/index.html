How to rescue a machine

Brush away dust, dissolve rust with solvent, free screws with the spanner, then turn copper plates. Join IN on the left to OUT on the right and test the power. A working route earns one star; clean half or all of the diamond-marked owner-request plates for two or three.

Drag over plates to clean; pass over a dusty plate again for its second layer. Keyboard: arrows move focus, 1–4 select tools without leaving your plate, Enter/Space use tool, Z undo an action or a whole cleaning drag (up to120 session actions). Tool changes and fault inspection keep history; reload, upgrades, restart and commission changes clear it. No clock, no lives, no hurry. Power tests are free, so experiment. After testing a working machine, Continue restoration keeps its circuit intact while you finish the marked service request; earned stars stay safe. Replay only awards coins for improving your best result.
