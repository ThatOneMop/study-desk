CAPTAIN’S NOTES

Good stowage is good sailing.

Pick a cargo card, rotate it with R if needed, then select its top-left hold cell. Review the footprint and before/after list, then press Stow load. Arrow keys move the hold selection. Both marked contracts must be aboard. Optional goods raise the delivery value. You can move a load by placing it again, or unload it.

Heavy cargo to port pulls the ship upward on the chart; starboard pulls downward. Loose cargo slides as the ship leans, increasing the list. Two lashings stop sliding and protect their cargo from impact damage. Fore-and-aft rows are packing space; port/starboard weight determines drift.

Cast off. Hold A / ← for port and D / → for starboard. W / ↑ increases sail; S / ↓ reduces it. Hold Space / Reef to slow and steady the ship. Use separate fingers on touch controls. The optional dotted course guide projects two seconds with your current helm and sail; amber is clear, coral warns of a collision or failed landing. It does not steer for you. Rocks and shores damage the hull and loose goods.

At the gold harbour mouth, line up and hold Reef. Arrive with both contracts to unlock the next passage. Earn a second seal for an intact hull and a third for bringing every offered cargo home intact. Best deliveries are ranked by seals, cargo value, then time; there is no countdown.

Deliver two passages to unlock the coastal cutter. Retry keeps your packing and best logbook entries. Reload restores the voyage paused. Focus loss releases controls and pauses.
