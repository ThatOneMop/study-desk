Soundings

Two lost beacons. A little fuel. Listen before you lower the hook.

Reading the water

Select a square, then Sound. Its number is the distance to the nearer of two beacons, counting horizontal and vertical steps, never diagonals. A reading of 0 means a beacon is there. Different buoys may hear different beacons. Bearings cost 2 fuel and report compass directions to both beacons, without distances or identities. North is up; NE means somewhere above and right, not necessarily a 45-degree line. Here means a beacon is in the selected square. Repeated directions mean both beacons lie in that direction. Choose cheap distances or the more expensive directional survey.

The notebook rules out impossible beacon pairs using your readings and recovery attempts. Dotted squares are still possible locations, not recommendations. A solid ring marks a location present in every remaining pair. Only recovery collects a beacon; its signal remains active afterwards.

You start with 14 fuel. Sound costs 1; Bearings cost 2; any recovery costs 3, even a miss. Keep enough fuel to recover both! No clock. Retry repeats this chart; New voyage changes the hidden locations, keeping sector unlocks and local best fuel records. Keyboard: Tab to chart buttons, Enter/Space to select, then activate Sound or Recover.
