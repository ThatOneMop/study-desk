Cairnfall

Make room. Let gravity do the rest.

How to stack

Choose a column and rotate the linked pair. Translucent stones show their landing spots. Horizontal pairs settle independently in their columns.

Four or more touching stones with the same letter clear together. Only edges connect; diagonals do not. Stones fall into the gaps, potentially clearing another group. Each chain wave multiplies its cleared-stone points: 4 stones on wave two score 8.

Exchange the current pair with the next to build a future chain. You start with two charges, may exchange once per drop, and regain one charge after a two-wave chain. Identical pairs cannot be exchanged. The drop forecast explains every wave before committing.

Reach the quarry's target before the bag runs out or stones reach the top row. There is no timer. Plan with the next three pairs. Retry keeps completed records.

Left/right arrows move; up rotates; down drops. Buttons also support Tab, Space and Enter. Every adjustment saves.
