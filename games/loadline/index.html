How to build a better bridge

Fill every segment between the banks, then test the truck. Timber is cheap; steel and truss tolerate heavier loads. Unsupported spans multiply the load. Braces shorten the span on both sides of a joint. Green segments have spare capacity, orange segments are near their limit, red segments accumulate damage and can buckle. The truck must cross the whole bridge.

Tap a segment to use the selected material. Select Support brace and tap a joint to toggle it; all edits refund their original cost. Keyboard: 1–5 tools, arrows select deck, Enter builds, B toggles a brace, Space tests, R returns to the blueprint, Escape pauses. Six deliveries are freely selectable.
