Choose lean and pace, read the forecast, then ride. Gaps need speed ≥3; roots need ≤2. Lean changes drift; Keep drift does not centre you.

Read the trail

There is no real-time timer. Choose lean and pace, check the exact forecast, then ride one section. Choices stay selected until changed. Left/right changes sideways drift by one; Keep drift keeps the current drift, it does not centre the bike.

Pedal raises speed, brake lowers it. Speed ranges from 1 to 4. Faster bends push you farther toward the outside edge. Stay between −4 and +4. Gaps need speed 3 or 4; roots need speed 1 or 2.

A scrape costs one of three bike-condition points and resets your line and speed. Finish with condition remaining, then improve your simulated descent time. Touch or Tab and Space/Enter operate all controls. Finish a trail to unlock its alternate lines. Their exact landing line and maximum drift are shown before riding. Root bypasses and gap bridges remove the terrain speed limit but add two time units; open shortcuts save one time unit and need speed ≥3. Every choice saves; restart keeps records.
