First shift

Move to a crate’s floor, load it, then deliver at its arrow destination. Bring the empty lift home after all three deliveries. You may park freight on other floors. Capacity is six tonnes; the cabin itself weighs two.

At ground level, choose a counterweight. Moving costs the weight imbalance plus one charge for friction. If gravity helps enough, the motor recovers charge instead. Read Up/Down forecasts before moving. Battery capacity is twelve.

Out of charge? Call the engineer; the dispatch stays playable, but each call adds100 record points. Each floor travelled adds one. Lower records are better. Try another dispatch after finishing, or replay to improve. Tab and Space operate controls; touch directly. No clock runs. Every action saves.
