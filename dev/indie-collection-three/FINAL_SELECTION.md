# Final slate selection — 5 November 2026

All 50 third-batch candidates have a playable version, a substantive rendered-play improvement pass and qualification evidence. This review uses their recorded mechanics, rendered tests and visual assessments; it is not a human enjoyment survey or a fresh playthrough of every game.

## Selection

Select **Mycelium Yard (66)** and **Bell Diver (68)** for final integration; both are now integrated and live. Mycelium combines job allocation, staggered growing, freshness, reinvestment and ordered buyer baskets; Bell Diver combines spatial air/load risk, banking, equipment purchases and optional combined-load charters. These add progression choices beyond short best-move puzzles. Both still have compact authored scope, so no duration claim is justified.

The two complement each other: relaxed production/business versus deliberate resource-risk exploration. Their existing qualification covers progression, failures/retry, save migration and keyboard/touch shells. Final release must also pass the current launcher and live-route checks.
