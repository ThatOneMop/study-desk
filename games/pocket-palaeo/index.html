Pick removes two soil layers quickly. When pale bone appears, switch to Brush: another pick strike cracks it. Drag over cells to work; each cell is worked once per stroke.

Clear the soil on and immediately beside a specimen. Place reusable braces on exposed bones, then choose a lifting point. Every unsupported link away from a support adds load; heavy bones carry extra. Choosing a lifting point shows exact loads and resulting condition before you confirm.
