How the stream works

Select a ground tile, then cut it down or fill it up by one height step. Cut soil goes into your bucket; filling spends it. The bucket holds four scoops. You cannot change rocks, the spring or root beds. Return all borrowed soil to the ground before finishing.

Water always goes to the lowest neighbouring ground that is strictly lower. Equal lowest neighbours split it evenly. It never crosses a flat step or climbs uphill. A hollow with no lower exit traps water. Root beds absorb arriving water. Each bed must receive its labelled share; spare water at one bed does not help the other.

Release water as often as you like: a dry bed does not destroy your layout. Reshape and try again. Preview is optional and costs nothing. Undo restores soil; retry preserves records. Finish a garden to unlock the next and try using fewer earthworks. There is no timer.

Keyboard: native Tab/Enter/Space for controls. On the page or terrain, C cuts, F fills, P releases water and Z undoes. Arrow keys move the selected plot. Shortcuts never override selectors or other buttons.
