Field notes

Nibs drink stored water, never the river. A paw pump fills tanks while the sources run. A stick dam holds a pool on its upstream side and lets the rest spill. A clay levee is a wall. A sluice gate is a lip you set: shut it when a sour tide is coming.

Footpaths must reach the district hearth. Frames off the path stay unbuilt. Stairs climb one floor. Platforms stack three high and water runs under them. Levees stack four high and water sits on top.

Logs become planks at a saw, and planks become gears at the press if a shaft brings power. A paddle wheel needs a sideways current. The Reed Circle keeps a fan turning in a dry spell. The Cog Lodge burns logs in an engine.

Reed kits need a spare lodge bed and a content colony. Cog kits come from a berry vat, and only when a bunk is free. Hunger is slow. Thirst is not.

Keys: WASD pans, Q and E rotate, the wheel zooms, 1–7 pick a build group, V water, R paths, G power, C sour, X removes, Space pauses, Esc opens this rest. On a touch screen, drag pans, a pinch zooms, and a tap places.
