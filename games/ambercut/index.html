THE LITTLE FIELD MUSEUM

Ambercut

Find the shape beneath the stone.

Field guide

Each cut costs one kit stroke. Chisel removes one layer from a five-square cross; brush removes one layer from just one square. Thick stone is safe to chisel. At one layer, a leaf trace reveals a fossil: brush it to avoid a fracture. Chisel can fracture thin fossils beside your target, even when the target is safe.

Expose every fossil square before 50 strokes or three fractures. You do not need to clear all stone. Preservation score is 100 minus strokes minus 12 per fracture. Three trays keep separate best scores. Tab and Space operate each square and tool; touch a tool then a square. No timer runs. Progress saves after every action.
