A reef is a neighbourhood.

Place up to eight habitat pieces. Meet all three goals, then complete your survey to unlock the next coast.

  • Coral: your largest side-connected coral colony counts. Diagonals do not connect.
  • Nurseries: each shell needs coral directly beside it, plus open water beside it connected to an edge. Shells on the edge already reach the sea.
  • Kelp: only side-connected groups of three or more count as forest.

Water flows only through empty squares. Stones and every habitat block it. Keep channels open!

Selective cuts: twice per reef, remove one coral, kelp or nursery square. This can reopen a sea channel or leave room for a later piece, but loses that habitat. Cuts do not advance the offer deck or refund planting. Preview the exact tradeoff, then Confirm cut; Undo restores it. Cuts remain available after the eighth planting, until you complete the survey.

Earn extra medals at the vitality targets. Replay any unlocked reef; your best survey is kept. Undo is free.

Keyboard: 1–3 choose, R rotates, X toggles selective cuts, arrow keys move on the grid, Enter previews / confirms, Z undoes. Mouse or touch: tap a square to preview, then Plant habitat. The preview reports exact changes, including lost nurseries. Show sea channels to see which empty squares reach the edge.
