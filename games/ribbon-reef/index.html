How to cast

Each post needs three exact catches. Silver fish have a stripe, amber fish a spot, and pearl fish two rings. Rays have diamond-shaped wings and must stay free. Coral must stay outside the net, including its faded safety circle.

Draw one simple loop around the requested fish. The rope must not cross itself, touch fish, or exceed your spool. Your next catch comes from the fish left in the water, so choose your groups carefully.

A wrong catch or coral snag releases everything and frays the rope. Three frays end the trip. You can undo any haul or retry with no record loss. A completed trip records all rope used, including released catches; lower is better.

Touch or mouse: drag in the water, lift to close, then Haul. Use Adjust corners to move or remove a corner without retracing the whole net. Keyboard: focus the water, use arrows to move the needle (Shift for fine steps), Space to start/close, Enter to haul, Escape to clear. No timer or automatic movement.
