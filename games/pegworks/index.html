Every bounce counts.

Break pegs to damage six workshop pests. Gold pegs hit hard; mint pegs repair you. Finish in a glowing pocket to multiply the shot.

How the workshop works

Drag on the board to aim and release to shoot, or use ← / → then Space. Each peg can score once per marble. Gold pegs deal triple damage; mint pegs also repair 3 health. Bottom pockets multiply that marble's damage by ×1, ×2 or ×3. The pest attacks after each marble; defeat it before you run out. Clear a shift to repair 14 health and pick one of six offered upgrades across common, rare and epic tiers. Six shifts win the run, and every third shift is a mini-boss that telegraphs a big slam. Pests vary: armoured foes blunt your marble unless you carry pierce, and splitters crack into two smaller pests. Escape pauses. R starts a fresh run after a result. Your best score saves on this device; a run does not save mid-shot.
