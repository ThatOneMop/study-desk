    How to play & controls

    Each auction has three lots. Select one and bid 0–6 coins. Bea and Otis show their bids beside every lot. A tie goes to you in auctions 1, 3, 5 and 7; rivals take ties in 2, 4, 6 and 8. The minimum winning bid button never places a bid.

    Only your chosen lot can be bought. The other lots leave with the rivals. Losing or passing uses that auction but costs no coins. A winning bid costs exactly your offer, so overbidding leaves less for later. Deliver any affordable commission between auctions for one coin back. Each commission can be delivered once; making things does not use an auction. You may still deliver after auction eight.

    Finish all three to win. Your best result for a fair favours fewer auctions, then more coins left. Undo is free, even after the result. Replay keeps records. A fair number produces repeatable offers and can be shared with a friend; these are variations of this small game, not separate campaigns.

    Tab/Enter/Space use every control. With page or lot focus: 1–3 select a lot, B bids, M sets its minimum, Z undoes. Native fields and buttons keep their usual keys. Pause freezes input. No clock.
