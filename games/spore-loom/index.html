The colony's rules

Tap empty soil to plant; tap your own spores to lift them. Pale-edged starter spores stay. Plant within your allowance, then grow. All cells change together: a living spore survives with 2 or 3 neighbours; empty soil sprouts with exactly 3. All eight surrounding cells count. Edges do not wrap.

Match every gold-ringed cell, with no extra spores, at the final generation. Replant restores your starting pattern so you can change it. No timer, lives or penalty for experimenting. Tab and Space/Enter operate each cell and control. Every change saves.

Cross a planting plot to unlock its disturbed colony. In tending mode, choose the timing and destination of one transplant between generations. Tap a live spore, then empty soil. Tap the selected spore again to cancel. Replant resets the disturbance and restores your transplant; earned seals stay.
