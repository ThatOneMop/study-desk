Your satchel

Shape your next draw

Keep coins or change your bag. Added ingredients stay; removed thorns never return. Maximum 15 ingredients.

How to brew

Draw without replacement. Leaf adds 1 coin; blossom adds 3. A brew containing both earns a 2-coin blend bonus. Mint cools bitterness by 1; thorn adds 2. At 4 bitterness the pot spoils and earns nothing. Bottle whenever you like to bank its value.

Between pots, spend earnings on blossoms for value, mint for cooling, or remove thorns for safety (one always stays). Each next pot refills from your improved bag. Earn personal brewing seals at 6, 9 and 12 coins in one pot. There is no clock and no real money.

Tab and Space operate every control. Draw order saves after every action: reloading does not reroll it. A new satchel resets coins and upgrades, keeping your best brew.
