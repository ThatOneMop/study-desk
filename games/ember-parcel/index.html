Before you continue

A living parcel

Every step on dry ground costs one warmth. Timber restores four after the step; water costs four (two with the wax cloak). Ice blocks you: melt it from beside it for three warmth. You must retain some warmth to melt.

A flare costs two warmth and consumes all adjacent timber, giving three warmth per timber. It also destroys adjacent gardens. Walking onto a garden burns it. Keep gardens alive and take efficient routes for higher records.

Pack mode collects adjacent timber for one warmth without moving. Carry up to two bundles, then Stoke one for four warmth anywhere without burning gardens. Packing costs more actions than walking onto timber, but lets you keep fuel for ice or water later. At zero warmth with a bundle left, stoke to continue.

Boardwalk mode (V) spends one packed bundle and one warmth to cover adjacent water or a garden without moving. The crossing costs one warmth to walk on and protects gardens from footsteps and flares. Choose between burning the bundle for four warmth or improving your route. Lift mode (C) returns an adjacent boardwalk to your bag for one warmth and one action. Cross it first, then lift it from the far side. The original garden or water returns, so a return trip becomes dangerous again. Keep it for protection or recover its timber for fuel; a full bag cannot collect it. Switch modes, then use a direction or tap an adjacent tile. Undo also removes a boardwalk and returns its cost.

No clock runs. Undo is always free, including after an ember goes out. The first hearth unlocks equipment; each delivery opens the next route. Replays improve records without farming currency.
