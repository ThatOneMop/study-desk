SMALL TEAMS. BIG BOUNCES.

Soccer Chaos

A scrappy little match where the ball never plays quite the same way twice.

Best of five rounds · nine pitch mutations

Make a little chaos.

Blue attacks the right goal, Coral attacks left. Score below the goal’s crossbar. A goal wins a round — first to three rounds wins the best-of-five match. A 3-2-1 kickoff countdown starts each round, and every round mutates the pitch: goal size, sliding goals, gravity, ball size, super-bouncy balls and wind all change.

Solo against the CPU, or bring a friend.

Choose your match, then jump in.

Your side of the court

Hold S (Coral: ↓) while hitting for a low kick. A D move · W jump · Space / X kick. Space / X kicks a nearby ball toward the other goal. W jumps for an aerial touch. Run into the ball to nudge it; kicks add the power. Every round the pitch mutates — bigger or smaller goals, goals that slide up and down, heavier gravity, super-bouncy balls and a curling crosswind. In solo the CPU keeper dives across its goal to block you.

A friend on the same keyboard

Choose Two players before starting. Coral moves with ← →, jumps with ↑ and uses Enter / L to kick. Esc pauses. Touch buttons support both sides. First to three rounds takes the match; solo wins save in this browser.
