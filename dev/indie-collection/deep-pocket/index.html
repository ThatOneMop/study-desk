First time at the lake?

Pick a bank and depth. The field guide predicts which fish you will hook. Each cast advances the tide; there are eight casts per field trip.

During a catch, choose Reel, Brace or Give line. The fish's next pull adds to your action's tension change. Reach 100 distance before beat 14, keeping tension strictly between 0 and 100. Slack, a snapped line or running out of beats loses the fish. Three escapes end the trip. There is no real-time clock.

Land a fish, then keep it for shells or release it for nursery goals. Either records its species. Fulfil all three goals and return. Earn a medal for finishing, another for no escapes, and another for finishing within the displayed cast target. Only better replays award more medals.

Medals buy equipment between trips. New equipment applies on the next trip or retry. Your journal and progress save in this browser when storage is available. Retry resets only the current trip.

Keyboard: Tab and Enter/Space for every control; R / B / E during a catch.
