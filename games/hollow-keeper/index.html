WELCOME, ROOTKEEPER

A maze that stays alive.

Keep the beetle nursery safe through two waves in each of five hollows. During preparation, choose a tool and a map cell, inspect its preview, then press Use selected tool. You have three soil edits per wave. Dig soft soil or pack an open tunnel; every entrance must keep a route to the nursery. Stone, entrances and nursery cannot be changed.

Thorn beds cost 2 resin, have three strikes per wave and deal 1 damage per strike. Reinforce for 2 resin to deal 2 damage. Reclaim a bed for its full cost. You can tend three beds. Between waves, all beds refill and you gain 4 resin.

Intruders follow the shortest open route, preferring north, east, south, then west when paths tie. One arrives every other march step, rotating through entrances. Each survivor reaching the nursery costs one heart. Armored grubs show their remaining health. A bed strikes only on entry, not while a creature waits on it.

During defense, drop fruit for 1 resin. Every intruder follows the fruit while it lasts; the first arrival eats it. Its scent fades after five march steps. Dropping fruit also advances one step. Use it to bend a route or lead an intruder back across a charged thorn bed.

Controls: 1 soil, 2 thorns, 3 reinforce, 4 reclaim, 5 fruit. Arrow keys move the map cursor; Enter selects a cell, then confirms its preview. L launches; Space marches; Z rewinds. Tap controls work too. Hold-repeat and shortcuts inside dialogs are ignored. P belongs to Study Desk.

Planning: dotted lines show deterministic routes. A tool preview shows the changed routes and supplies without changing the defense. The scout report simulates the remaining wave with no more fruit or changes; it reports expected brood hearts and escapes. During an attack, selecting a cell pauses auto march. Cancel leaves the defense untouched. Empty thorn beds turn dark and show a cross.

Three seals require all hearts and no more than two terrain edits. Progress and best records save in this browser. Auto march is optional and never resumes itself after reload. No sound is required.
