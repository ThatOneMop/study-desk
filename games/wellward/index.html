FIELD NOTES

Slow hands, safe cargo.

Launch, then hold A / D (or ← / →) to slide the overhead winch. The bucket swings beneath it. Hold S / ↓ to lower, W / ↑ to haul. Space steadies the swing and slows the controls for careful positioning.

Bring the bucket close to a glowing relic, steady it, then press E or Lift relic. You begin with two cargo hooks. Return near the central surface landing and press Bank cargo. Only banked relics and brass survive a broken strap or an empty return.

At the landing you may pack one free repair kit. It occupies one cargo hook until consumed. After an impact clears, steady below 20 speed and press R or Repair to mend one damaged strap. Repair pauses the descent so you can plan safely; resume when ready. You cannot repair a broken bucket, refill a kit underwater or exceed three straps. Returning resets the kit choice; banked relics stay safe.

Below the waterline, F fills the bucket with stabilising ballast. Press again anywhere to drain. A heavy bucket swings less in the current, but hauls more slowly.

Each well has a seal that opens the next. Recover all four seals to finish; all sixteen relics complete the cabinet. Buy winch speed, padding or cargo hooks with brass. There is no countdown or repeat-collection grind.

Choose a relic or the surface landing in “Sketch a route”. The dotted clearance sketch shows a way through the shoulders; you still operate the winch. Lift relic glows when distance and swing are safe.

On touch, hold the large buttons; two fingers can combine sliding, hauling and steadying. Focus loss pauses. Reload returns to the exact saved descent, paused.
