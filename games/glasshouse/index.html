Both parents must be mature and side-by-side. Each can cross once per day. Colours combine; equal heights stay equal. Mixed heights make tall offspring on even days, low on odd days.

First morning under glass?

Select a seed packet and an empty pot, then Sow. Water holds two days of moisture. Each day gives six water units; pollination also uses one. Only Advance day moves time. Lift and move plants into empty pots for one water; moving preserves growth and moisture, and changes shade immediately.

Each plant needs three suitable, watered growing days to mature. Gold and amber need bright light; blue and violet prefer shade or soft light; jade needs soft light. Rose and prism accept soft or bright light. Tall plants reduce light directly below by one step. Frosted panes also reduce light.

The greenhouse has one linen shade. Hanging or moving it over any pot costs one of today’s water units and lowers that pot’s current and future window light by one step until moved. It can prepare an empty pot or protect a planted one, but the water tradeoff means tall-neighbour shade still matters.

To breed, select a mature plant and Choose as pollen parent, then select a mature neighbour and Cross. One seed combines their colour lineages. Gold + rose makes amber; gold + blue makes jade; rose + blue makes violet. A hybrid crossed with its missing colour makes prism.

Deliver the exact requested height and colour from a mature pot. Two consecutive dry days cause wilting. The exhibition stays open through its final day; trying to advance beyond that day ends the attempt. Retry resets this garden, not earlier medals, records or discovered lineages.
