Gummy Bay

Playable build is v165. The interface font is Nunito (SIL Open Font License 1.1; licence bundled in games/gummy-bay/assets/fonts/OFL.txt). Rigged townsfolk models are by Quaternius, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain) and obtained via poly.pizza; they were recoloured and optimised for the game. City buildings use Kenney's City Kit (Commercial 2.1 and Suburban 2.0), also CC0 1.0 (www.kenney.nl), tinted and merged for the game. Full list in games/gummy-bay/CREDITS.md. Existing gummybay-v3 saves are retained. Blender sources and version notes are kept beside the game and are not part of the published site. See GUMMY_BAY_UPDATE.md. Third-party engine notices remain alongside the game.

Ashwake

Original Study Desk game. A lantern walks a drowned orchard through two nights, the Bell Warden and then the Hollow Bell. The Wickling is a second keeper. Code-drawn art and synthesized sound; no third-party assets or branding. Progress, achievements and play time are stored only under study-desk-ashwake-v1.

Lane Eight

Original swimming career game created for the user. Canvas stadium, swimmers, water and SVG training artwork are original. Six fictional clubs, manager decisions and direct keyboard/touch racing. No Michael Phelps branding, likeness, game assets or Kinect integration. Existing career save format retained; export/import moves careers between origins.

Winterfold

Original trail-map resort game. Code, pines, interface and synthesized sound were made for Study Desk. No assets or names were taken from other ski games. Hills are fictional. Progress is stored only under study-desk-winterfold-v1. See games/winterfold/CREDITS.md.

Stencil Studio

Original third-collection printmaking game. Code, CSS paper and ink artwork, and SVG cover created for Study Desk; no external assets. Three compact commissions, selective ink lifting and best-operation records. Saves use study-desk-stencil-studio-v1.

Moon Dial

Original Study Desk code, CSS clockwork and SVG cover art. Three compact coupled-dial mechanisms with reversible turn rehearsal, local progress and best-turn records. No third-party assets.

Pebble Rink

Pocket Regatta

Original Study Desk code, CSS sailing chart and SVG cover artwork. Three compact wind-aware buoy courses with editable route drafts, undo and local pennant records. No third-party assets. Saves use study-desk-pocket-regatta-v1.

Crate & Cushion

Needlepath

Original Study Desk stitching-route puzzle. Code, authored sampler geometry, SVG embroidery art and CSS are original; no external game or asset pack. Three compact samplers with seamless and knotwork studies, saved patterns and optional tracing. No content-duration claim.
