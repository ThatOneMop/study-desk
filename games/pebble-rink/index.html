    Leave your stones in the house

    Release three stones, then meet the rink’s score target. The inner ring scores 3, middle ring 2 and outer ring 1. Only resting positions count; later stones can bump earlier ones.

    Use the optional forecast to see curved paths, bank rebounds and where every stone will rest. Hollow circles are predicted positions; solid stones are your saved positions. Adjusting controls updates the forecast without spending a stone. Hide it for unassisted play. Choose an angle and weight. Curl bends the early part of the shot. Stones slow on the ice and bounce from the bank and islands. Start around weight 73, then adjust your line. There is no countdown.

    Tap the ice to choose a line, or use sliders and keys. Arrow left/right changes angle; up/down changes weight. Space releases. Replay last shot watches its collisions again without changing your score or save. The shot notes show which stones gained or lost points. Undo restores earlier positions and recalls the removed shot’s angle, weight and curl, ready to adjust. Retry keeps earned club cards.

    Shots save their resolved result immediately. Reloading skips any remaining animation, keeping the same outcome. Pause freezes animation; returning from another tab stays paused.
