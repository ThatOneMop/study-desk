First night on the job

Use WASD or arrows to thrust. You keep drifting after release. Hold Space (or Brake) to slow down. Get within 75 metres of an object and press E (or Couple); tow it into the striped beacon. While coupled, hold Q (or Winch −) to shorten the cable for a stronger pull and tighter turn, or R (or Winch +) to pay it out for a gentler, wider tow. The approach board shows cable length and live tension. Objects dock automatically inside 67 metres and below 38 m/s. The other approach cues separate range, cargo speed and whether the load is closing or drifting away; guidance is not autopilot. Release with E if you need to reposition. No fuel and no countdown: take your time. The blue line shows your current drift. The manifest gives each object’s distance and compass bearing. On small screens the camera follows your tug; edge labels locate offscreen cargo and dock.

Three medals per job: recover everything, avoid high-speed tug impacts, and beat the optional efficiency target. Replays award only new medals. Escape pauses. Touch: hold the Winch − or Winch + button; tap Couple.
