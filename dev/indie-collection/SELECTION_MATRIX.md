# Indie collection finalist matrix · 22 October 2026

This is an evidence-backed **preselection**, not a publication decision. All nine finalists are finished development candidates with an initial playable, at least one meaningful improvement pass, complete source, engine/DOM/browser suites and campaign evidence in the ledger. Their `selected` flags remain false and their `publishedUrl` fields remain null until scoped integration against fresh `main` passes combined catalogue and deployment checks.

No numerical “fun score” is used: the available play evidence is agent-driven and cannot stand in for independent human preference testing. The slate instead optimizes demonstrated depth, control polish, reliability, the requested taste profile and non-overlapping core loops.

## Finalist slate

Together these finalists explicitly cover fishing, colony building, manual craft, roguelike/extraction and action, while adding ferry logistics, stealth and breeding. The machine-readable companion is `finalists.json`; `tests/indie-selection-gate.py` rejects unfinished, duplicated, untested or prematurely public entries.

## Decision rule

Only a finalist that survives fresh-main source copying, catalogue navigation, save/restart, keyboard/touch-emulated controls, combined repository tests and deployment verification may change to `selected: true`. A failure keeps it development-only and promotes the best-fitting reserve rather than lowering the gate.
