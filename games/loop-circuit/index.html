How to ride

↑ / W / Space (or hold the canvas) is the throttle. ↓ / S is the brake. ← / A and → / D lean the bike back and forward — hold back to wheelie or rotate mid-air, forward to bring the nose down before a landing.

Balance is everything: on a climb keep the front from lifting too far, and in the air a touch of lean sets your landing angle. A wheelie, a head-down landing or a fall into a creek ends the run instantly. It restarts on its own after a slow-motion beat, or press R to go again right away. Esc pauses, Circuits returns to the menu.

Each circuit has three medal times. Your best time and your sound choice save in this browser under loop-circuit-v1. Everything — the physics, the four hand-built circuits, the bike, the sound — is original and made here; no third-party assets are used.
