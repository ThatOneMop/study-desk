Reading the water

Select a square, then Sound. Its number is the distance to the nearer of two beacons, counting horizontal and vertical steps, never diagonals. A reading of 0 means a beacon is there. Different buoys may hear different beacons. Bearings cost 2 fuel and report compass directions to both beacons, without distances or identities. North is up; NE means somewhere above and right, not necessarily a 45-degree line. Here means a beacon is in the selected square. Repeated directions mean both beacons lie in that direction. Area sweep costs 1 fuel and counts how many of BOTH beacons are within two horizontal/vertical grid steps of your selected square, including the square itself. It reports 0, 1 or 2, not their distances or identities. Toggle the footprint to inspect its area before spending fuel. Choose nearest distance, area counts or the more expensive directional survey.

The notebook rules out impossible beacon pairs using your readings and recovery attempts. Dotted squares are still possible locations, not recommendations. A solid ring marks a location present in every remaining pair. Only recovery collects a beacon; its signal remains active afterwards.

You start with 14 fuel. Sound and Area sweep cost 1; Bearings cost 2; any recovery costs 3, even a miss. Keep enough fuel to recover both! No clock. Retry repeats this chart; New voyage changes the hidden locations, keeping sector unlocks and local best fuel records. Keyboard: Tab into the chart, use arrow keys to select a square, then Tab to the actions. Enter/Space activates buttons. Planning and moving the selection cost no fuel.
