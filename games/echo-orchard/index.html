LEAVE A LITTLE LIGHT BEHIND

Echo Orchard

Walk once. Let your footsteps lend a hand.

Reach the golden exit. Stand on leaf plates a/b to open gates A/B. Imprint your walk to leave an echo holding a plate while you move on.

How echoes work

Each move or Wait advances every echo one saved footprint. Echoes ignore gates and remain at their final footprint. Imprinting returns you to the entrance and restarts all echoes. You have two echo slots. Record the first plate route, imprint it, then record a route to the second plate while the first echo opens its gate.

Gate checks use positions before the step; wait until a gate opens before entering. A blocked move does not consume a turn. Erase removes only the newest echo and returns you to the entrance. Every choice saves. Restart preserves records. Touch or Tab and Space/Enter operate the controls. Arrow keys move; W waits, I imprints and U erases. No timer or lives.

After crossing a garden, try Duet. Echoes walk their recorded path forward and backward forever. Waiting on a plate before imprinting records extra plate time, widening the gate window. The forecast shows which gates are open now and over the next seven beats, assuming you stay still. Gate checks happen before a step. Restart or erase to redesign a route; return to gentle mode any time.
