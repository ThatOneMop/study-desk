Mycelium Yard

Grow a colourful harvest in ten mornings. Spend three jobs each day; moisture grows crops, excess rain rots them. There is no real-time clock.

    Growing guide

    Choose a bed, then a crop. Seeds include two moisture. Each evening, rain arrives first; growing beds use one moisture to gain one growth. Ready crops do not drink. More than three moisture causes one rot; two rot loses the bed. Vent removes two moisture from every planted bed. Ripe crops lose one coin each evening and spoil on the third. Harvest or compost frees a bed. Water, planting, harvest, compost and vent each cost one job. End day is free.

    Honey fan: 2 coins, 2 growth, 5 sale. Blue bell: 3 coins, 3 growth, 9 sale. Rose cup: 3 coins, 2 growth, 7 sale (market two). Sales count toward your goal even when reinvested. Three markets unlock in order. Choose one of two buyers in each market. Qualifying harvests automatically fill the current basket; extra crops sell normally and do not carry into the next basket. Buyer bonuses count as sales and can be reinvested. Complete both baskets for a permanent badge. Replay to serve the other buyer or improve sales. Buyer choice locks at the first harvest after choosing. Old saves gain no retroactive badges. These are fictional crop rules, not cultivation advice.

    1–6 select beds; H harvests, W waters, V vents, N ends day. Tab/Enter works on all buttons. P is reserved for Study Desk. Progress saves after each action.
