How to write your way out

Choose a card, then a highlighted square. Only the landing square collects pages, wells or blot damage. Recover at least three of four pages and reach ⇧. Shelves block straight strokes; Fold slips diagonally. Ink wells refill once.

Every action uses one turn. Resting restores ink but moves the closing bell closer. Every fourth turn, draft a new card. Your discarded cards cycle back. No real-time clock is running.

Earn one seal for escape, one for all four pages, and one for a careful quick route. Replay only rewards a better result. Upgrades apply to the next volume/retry.

Keyboard: 1–3 select card · arrows move map focus · Enter/Space activates · R rests. Native Tab also works.
