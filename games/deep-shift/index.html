Field manual & controls

Arrow keys / WASD drill adjacent tiles. Hold a direction to keep drilling. Hard rock and precious seams take several hits, costing oxygen per stroke. Gas pockets damage your hull and vent oxygen. Each shift generates a fresh deterministic mine. Dark tiles are unexplored; a survey pulse reveals seven tiles around you and costs 10 oxygen.

Return to the surface row to bank 100% of your haul. Meet the quota in one safe return to advance the contract. Emergency lift banks 65% but never completes a contract. Hull failure, running out of oxygen or abandoning a shift loses that shift's haul. Your bank and purchased upgrades are retained. Six contract tiers are included; after the final quota you can keep mining. Workshop upgrades apply at the start of the next shift.

Space: scan. E: emergency lift. Escape: pause. Touch: hold the arrow buttons below the mine. Progress saves in this browser; there are no offline earnings. The mine pauses while hidden.
