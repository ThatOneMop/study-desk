The pilot’s notes

Sail with arrow keys or the direction buttons. Pick up every gold parcel, then reach the striped dock. The boat moves only when you act.

Sand channels (≋) open at low tide; blue channels (≈) open at high tide. Change the tide with T while on deep water. A boat in a shallow channel cannot change the tide.

Sail onto the brass wheel (⚙) to open every lock (▥). Locks stay open. Undo any action with Z. Restart only resets this route.

Every move and tide change counts. Beat the route’s turn target for a gold seal; all successful deliveries unlock the next route.
