A SMALL STUDY IN GRAVITY

Tumbleglass

Tilt the whole tray. Nest each glass bead in its matching numbered socket.

At the glassmaker’s bench

All loose beads slide as far as they can. The bead nearest the tilted edge moves first. Walls and other beads stop them. A bead locks immediately when it passes over its own numbered socket. Locked beads never move — plan which one to settle first. Other sockets do not stop it.

Use the arrow keys on the page or tray, or the four tilt buttons. The small number on a bead matches its socket, so colour is optional. Undo is unlimited; retry keeps your best records. Solve a tray to unlock the next, then try fewer tilts. No clock and no lives.
