    Welcome to the night shift.

    Deliver the manifest in order. Cargo travels at the bottom of the hook. The crane uses discrete moves: take as long as you like.

    Balance = load weight × reach + 3 × ballast + wind. Keep it between −5 and +5. Ballast moves physically left with Q and right with E, countering cargo on the opposite side. Each unsafe action causes one overload; three stop the job. An empty hook uses ballast + wind.

    Moving sideways adds two swing. Raising, lowering or shifting ballast removes one. Steady cable removes two. Glass needs zero swing; other cargo shows its safe limit.

    Land at the stack's height in a marked bay. A supporting crate must carry the total weight above it. A glass lantern supports nothing. Lift one level above a stack before moving over it.

    Every control previews its resulting balance (B) and swing (S). Orange warnings mean an overload or damaged landing. Invalid moves cost nothing. The landing strip shows each marked bay’s exact height and whether its stack can carry your load.

    Padded cradles: Each job has two. At the empty centre dock, press C to fit or remove one before hooking. Fitting costs a move; the supply is spent on pickup. A cradled load tolerates three extra swing but weighs one tonne more while carried, so rebalance before travelling. The cradle leaves with delivery and adds no weight to the stack. It cannot prevent overloads. Undo reverses its use; restart restores the job supply.

    Lift rehearsal: Try the next delivery using the normal keyboard or tap controls. The scene shows your preview, while your saved attempt and records stay unchanged. Undo revises the plan; discard restores the starting position. Apply commits every planned move with its normal costs. The preview stops after one delivery. A route stopped by overloads must be revised before applying. Reload discards the rehearsal.

    Medals: complete the job; complete without damage or overloads; do both within the move target. Records keep your best result, then fewest moves. Replay unlocked jobs freely.

    Keys: WASD or arrows = hoist/trolley; Q/E = ballast; Space = hook/release; F = steady; C = fit/remove cradle at dock; Z = undo. All controls also work by tap. P is reserved for Study Desk.
