Tumbleglass

Glassmaker’s studies

Try a different technique on each tray. Every earned study keeps its best winning route; retry and undo keep your studies.

At the glassmaker’s bench

All loose beads slide as far as they can. The bead nearest the tilted edge moves first. Walls and other beads stop them. A bead locks immediately when it passes over its own numbered socket. Locked beads stay put during tilts — plan which one to settle first. Other sockets do not stop it. Once per attempt, you may clamp one loose bead in place for a single tilt. Choose it before tilting; the other beads still slide. Save the clamp for a tricky turn, or solve without it. A tilt that moves nothing does not spend the clamp. Undo restores it; retry gives it back.

Use the arrow keys on the page or tray, or the four tilt buttons. The small number on a bead matches its socket, so colour is optional. Undo is unlimited; retry keeps your best records. Solve a tray to unlock the next, then try fewer tilts. No clock and no lives. Once per attempt, the bead lift can move any bead one cell into an empty space. Select the bead and a direction; all other beads stay still. This costs one move, spends the lift; the bead locks only if placed in its own socket. Walls and beads block lifting; invalid directions spend nothing. Selecting a lift clears the clamp and vice versa. Undo restores the lift, and retry restores both tools. Optional Check tray searches all legal tilts, the unused clamp and bead lift. It shows one move on a shortest remaining route, or explains that undo is needed even if beads can still move. It never changes your layout, selected tool or save. Guidance clears after a successful tilt, undo or restart.
