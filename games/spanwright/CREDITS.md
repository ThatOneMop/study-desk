# Spanwright credits

Spanwright is an original browser game made for Study Desk.

- Code, illustration, interface, and level layout are original.
- Sound and music are synthesised in the page. No recorded samples.
- No art, audio, text, or level geometry is taken from any commercial bridge game.
- The material strengths and prices recorded in `docs/spanwright-research.md` are public figures used as a mechanics reference. The presentation around them is not.

Progress is stored in this browser under `study-desk-spanwright-v1`.
