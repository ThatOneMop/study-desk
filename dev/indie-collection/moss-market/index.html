A little guide to barter

Each visitor has two bundles but will make only one accepted trade this market. Inspect their values: a timber might be worth 3 to Fern, but only 1 as a stall reserve. Put any owned goods on the offer tray with + / −, choose a bundle and barter. Their valuation must meet the ask; excess valuation is overpayment. Three declined offers close the market. A full shelf blocks a trade without using patience.

Craft tea, balm, vessels and lamps from stock. Deliver community orders in any order, but remember delivered goods cannot be offered later. Your final order closes the stall immediately. Extra seals reward fair trades and a useful reserve of stock. Six short handmade markets have different starting stock, shelf space and order demands; no wall-clock timer.

Draft offers and selected visitors are saved as you adjust them. Recipes/deliveries/committed trades clear the tray to prevent stale offers. Enter/Space activate native controls; arrow keys move visitor focus. Each game uses its own browser save. This is a development prototype, not a real-world pricing model.
