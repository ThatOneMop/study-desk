Your satchel

How to brew

Draw without replacement. Leaf adds 1 coin; blossom adds 3. A brew containing both earns a 2-coin blend bonus. Mint cools bitterness by 1 and stores one reserve (maximum one). Spend that reserve before drawing to lower bitterness by 2 at a cost of 2 brew value; thorn adds 2. At 4 bitterness the pot spoils and earns nothing. Bottle whenever you like to bank its value.

Between pots, spend earnings on blossoms for value, mint for cooling, or remove thorns for safety (two always stay; old one-thorn saves are preserved). Each next pot refills from your improved bag. Earn personal brewing seals at 6, 9 and 12 coins in one pot. Earn a Cooling seal by bottling at least 6 after spending mint, or a Bold seal by bottling at least 9 with bitterness 2 or more. Each seal grants 4 coins once and survives new satchels. There is no clock and no real money.

Tab and Space operate every control. Draw order saves after every action: reloading does not reroll it. A new satchel resets coins and upgrades, keeping your best brew.
