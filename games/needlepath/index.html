Thread journal

Original SVG & CSS artwork · Three compact samplers · Two stitching studies each

Follow the needle.

Begin at green pin 1. On the front, stitch to a pin in the same row. On the back, stitch to a pin in the same column. Every stitch turns the needle to the other face.

Stitch into every flower pin, then into the gold finish. Plain pins are optional: use them to change direction. Thread can pass over a pin without visiting it; only the chosen end of a stitch counts. Never visit a pin twice or overlap thread on the same face. Front and back threads may cross.

Each grid interval costs one thread. Once per sampler you can tie a knot for two thread and turn the needle without moving. A knot can rescue a route, but sometimes a longer route uses less thread than the knot.

Each sampler has two studies: finish without a knot, or work a useful knot into your route. Both have their own best pattern and fine-thread target. Choose a study to focus the optional advice. Tracing your saved pattern lays a pale numbered route under your current stitches; it never plays for you. Your normal ribbons and unlocks stay intact.

The optional thread guide checks whether a finish remains, then suggests one move toward the shortest finish and forecasts its ribbon. It never moves for you, spends thread or changes your record. Hide it to solve unaided; it starts hidden on reload.

There is no clock. Undo is unlimited, including after a dead end or a finish. Retry keeps your ribbons. Completing each sampler unlocks the next. Shorter completed routes improve your record.

Tap or click a pin. Tab reaches every pin; arrow keys move focus between nearby pins and Enter or Space stitches. K ties a knot, U undoes. All actions save automatically when storage is available.
