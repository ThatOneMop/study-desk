THE CROSSING NEEDS A KEEPER

Lead from the front.
Let your creeps take the tower.

Green waves march from your citadel every 14 seconds. Stay behind them near enemy defenses, cast your three abilities, and spend earned gold on upgrades. The enemy hero has the same plan.

WASD / click move · Hold J / Space attack · Q shockwave · R restore · E dash

A lane you can read

Move with WASD / arrows, or click / tap a destination. Hold J / Space / Attack to strike the nearest enemy within your yellow range ring. Q shocks all nearby enemies, R heals you and nearby allies, and E dashes toward your movement direction. All three have visible cooldowns.

Red defenses target the closest unit. Let your creeps enter first. Your citadel and enemy citadel are protected until their tower falls. Defeat the enemy citadel to win; your hero respawns after eight seconds, but your citadel cannot.

Win the little economy

Earn gold and experience when enemy creeps or the enemy hero fall near you. Buy permanent match upgrades below. Your level rises every 120 experience, increasing attack and health. Return near your green citadel to recover health. Enemy waves become tougher with time. Every third wave includes a large gold-rimmed siege creep that deals double damage to defenses. Escort yours or intercept theirs.

The opposing hero retreats to heal, pursues exposed targets, and returns after defeat. Match upgrades reset each game. Wins and your fastest victory save on this device.
