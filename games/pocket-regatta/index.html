    Sail around the numbered buoys

    Cross each buoy square in order. Your boat cannot sail directly into the wind: use diagonal tacks to travel upwind. North is the top of the chart.

    You may turn up to 90° per move. Short sail moves one square; full sail moves two. Every crossed square counts, including buoys. The dotted preview shows the actual route. Grounding on rocks or the edge costs one hull and stops the boat before the obstacle.

    Three groundings end the crossing. Undo restores the last tack, including its hull and buoy progress. There is no timer. You can draft up to six tacks without moving: each new heading is checked from the previous planned endpoint. Gold squares show the draft, and the diamond is its endpoint. Edit any draft tack to change its heading or sail size without discarding the later route. The revised route is checked again: invalid later turns are marked for repair, and sailing is blocked until the whole draft is valid. Keep original tack cancels the edit. Remove or clear the draft freely. Sail next planned tack commits only the first tack; the rest remain a draft. Drafts are not saved and are cleared by undo, confirmed restart or course change. Try full sail on clear reaches and short sail for precision around rocks and buoys. Finish to unlock the next course; replay for a lower move record.

    Select heading buttons or keys 1–8. Enter while the chart has focus sails. Progress and sail settings save on this device.
