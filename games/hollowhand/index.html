Your deck

How to play

Play cards in any order using three energy each turn. Kindle costs zero and boosts your next attack this turn. Guard blocks the enemy's shown damage. Healing cannot exceed 24 health. Armor reduces each attack, so larger attacks can be efficient. Briar thorns reflect damage even on a finishing attack; guard first. The Lantern heals when its attack breaks through your guard. Read the wind draws two cards and grants two guard.

End turn to face the enemy, discard unused cards and draw five. Guard and sparks reset. Empty draw piles reshuffle the discard pile. Played cards return on later turns; Read the wind draws immediately and may reshuffle. Defeat four guardians, choosing a deck change after each of the first three. Victory records persist through restart.

Tab and Space operate every control. No clock runs. Progress saves after every card; reload preserves the draw order. This is a compact prototype, not a long campaign.
