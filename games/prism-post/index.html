A little light, delivered carefully. Turn the mirrors to bring a beam to every lantern.

How to deliver

The beam starts at the gold arrow. Click a mirror to switch between / and \. Light every round lantern at once.

Use arrow keys to move across the board and Enter or Space to turn a mirror. Z undoes a turn. There is no timer.

Finish without hints in no more turns than the number of mirrors for a gold stamp. You can replay any open delivery.
