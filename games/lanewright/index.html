How the lanes work

Houses are circles and hold two cars. Shops are squares. A car only collects a pin from a shop of the same colour, then drives home. Each pin is one trip.

A square shop can hold seven pins. A round shop, ten. Past that, a ring starts. If the ring completes, the city stops. Cars already out prefer a shop whose ring is running. Bringing the pins back under the cap lets the ring fade.

Drag along the grid to spend road tiles. Diagonals are allowed. Right-drag, or Erase, lifts a tile back into the pile. Bridge and Tunnel are counters, not tools: a crossing spends one on its own when the road meets water or a hill. A motorway is a fast raised thread that hops roads and water, not hills. A circle keeps a junction moving. A light lets one axis through at a time.

Sunday pauses the week and offers two supplies. Daily and weekly lanes share a seed for that day or that week. The sketchbook never runs out and never stops.

Keys: 1 road, 2 motorway, 3 bridge count, 4 tunnel count, 5 circle, 6 light, X erase, Z undo, Y redo, Space pauses the clock, Esc opens this pause, arrows pan, minus and plus zoom. On a phone the map starts zoomed in: drag empty ground to pan, pinch to zoom. Erase lifts a tile.
