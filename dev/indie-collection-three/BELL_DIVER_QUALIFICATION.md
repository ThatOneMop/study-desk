## Fixed rule

Qualification review found that arriving at the bell banked cargo before checking whether the final ascent had enough oxygen. A diver with1 air and a heavy bag could pay a2-air ascent and still receive salvage. A failing test reproduced the defect. The engine now banks only when the arrival air is non-negative; otherwise rescue drops unbanked cargo. Returning with exactly enough air remains valid. Existing saved banked progress is preserved.
