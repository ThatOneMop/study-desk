How to play

Space, W, ↑, click or tap leaps. Hold to keep leaping the instant you land — buffered jumps and coyote time forgive a slightly early or late press. Esc pauses, R restarts the run, Levels returns to the menu.

One touch of a spike, a block's face or a gap ends the run instantly, and it restarts straight away — your best distance is already saved. Red ✕ marks show where you have fallen before. Progress is your farthest distance as a percentage, saved per level in this browser.

Practise mode (toggle on any level card) adds checkpoints, so a fall returns you to the last one instead of the start. Everything — the music, the art and the levels — is original and drawn in the browser; no third-party assets are used.
