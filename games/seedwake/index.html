Choose a bed, then sow all its seeds one by one around the ring. Only the last landing can bloom.

Before you sow

How the garden works

Sowing lifts every seed in the chosen bed and drops one in each following bed, clockwise. Large handfuls can travel more than one lap, including through their starting bed. An anticlockwise sow uses one gust. A clockwise sow uses none.

The final bed blooms only when its resulting seed count is exactly its printed “Bloom at” number. That bed’s seeds are spent, and its flower goes into the bouquet. Passing through a bed never harvests it. A flower can still bloom after its bouquet request is filled: decide whether spending those seeds helps your remaining route.

Fill every request before the handful allowance runs out. There is no clock. Running out of seeds also ends the garden. Undo works after either outcome and returns spent gusts. Retry keeps your fewest-handful records. Completed gardens unlock the next one; revisit any unlocked garden to find a more efficient route.

Keyboard: Tab, Enter and Space operate every control. On the page or garden beds, 1–8 select a bed, Left/Right follow the ring, R changes direction, S sows and Z undoes. Shortcuts leave other controls alone.
