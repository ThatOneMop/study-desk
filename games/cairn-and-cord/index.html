FIELD NOTES / 01

A little higher, a little wiser.

Tap or click a hold, then Climb to hold. You can reach adjacent holds, including diagonals. Tab and Enter work on every control.

Each move uses grip. Diagonals cost two, straight moves one; rough holds add two. Wind adds grip cost only on exposed holds. If you run out of grip, you slip back to your latest anchor. Long falls cost two hearts; short falls cost one. Three lost hearts ends the climb.

Pay out rope until it reaches the next hold. Reel in spare slack to shorten falls. Set anchors at marked sound rock with one of three pitons. Rope runs through every anchor; on broad ledges, Move belay here reclaims your rope and pitons.

Rest on ledges refills grip. Elsewhere, two chalk portions each restore five grip. Reach the summit to open the next route. Bring the side-path cairn stone and avoid slips to earn all three badges. Replay for fewer climbing moves.

Selecting a hold draws a dotted rope forecast and shows exact grip, hearts and wind after your move. A red warning explains slips before you commit. Rope adjustments never spend a climbing move. Undo reverses your last action, including a fall, until you reach the summit.

Every action saves locally. Restart keeps summit records. New expedition book asks before clearing only this game.
