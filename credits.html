Gummy Bay

Playable build is v165. The interface font is Nunito (SIL Open Font License 1.1; licence bundled in games/gummy-bay/assets/fonts/OFL.txt). Rigged townsfolk models are by Quaternius, released under CC0 1.0 (public domain) and obtained via poly.pizza; they were recoloured and optimised for the game. City buildings use Kenney's City Kit (Commercial 2.1 and Suburban 2.0), also CC0 1.0 (www.kenney.nl), tinted and merged for the game. Full list in games/gummy-bay/CREDITS.md. Existing gummybay-v3 saves are retained. Blender sources and version notes are kept beside the game and are not part of the published site. See GUMMY_BAY_UPDATE.md. Third-party engine notices remain alongside the game.

Ashwake

Original Study Desk game. A lantern walks a drowned orchard through two nights, the Bell Warden and then the Hollow Bell. The Wickling is a second keeper. Code-drawn art and synthesized sound; no third-party assets or branding. Progress, achievements and play time are stored only under study-desk-ashwake-v1.

Winterfold

Original trail-map resort game. Code, pines, interface and synthesized sound were made for Study Desk. No assets or names were taken from other ski games. Hills are fictional. Progress is stored only under study-desk-winterfold-v1. See games/winterfold/CREDITS.md.

Spanwright

Original bridge-building game. Code, levels, interface and procedural illustration are original. Sound is synthesised in the page. No commercial bridge-game art, audio, text or level geometry is included. Material numbers used as a mechanics reference are cited in docs/spanwright-research.md. Saves use study-desk-spanwright-v1. Notes in games/spanwright/CREDITS.md.

Lanewright

Original road-drawing game. Code, cities, interface and procedural illustration are original. Sound is synthesised in the page. No commercial road-game art, audio, text or city names are included. Mechanics notes are in docs/lanewright-research.md. Saves use study-desk-lanewright-v1. Notes in games/lanewright/CREDITS.md.

Creekhold

Original river-colony game. Code, maps, interface, illustration and synthesized sound were made for Study Desk. No art, audio, text or level geometry was taken from other colony games. Mechanics notes, used only as a reference, are in docs/creekhold-research.md. Saves use study-desk-creekhold-v1. See games/creekhold/CREDITS.md.

Numberworks

Original mathematical factory game, code and artwork. Optional machinery modelled in Blender. Inspired by arithmetic factory games; no Beltmatic assets or source used. Imported at the user’s explicit request on 4 November 2026.

Study Desk does not host or modify their files; cover images are loaded from the catalogue. These games need internet, keep their own saves, and show advertising from the catalogue inside the game.
