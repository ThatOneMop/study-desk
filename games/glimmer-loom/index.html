Try a chain of mirror changes before spending placements. Solid light is your saved board; dashed light is your sketch. Applying uses just its first change.

    Select a square, then place a mirror. / turns east to north; \\ turns east to south. Rays can cross on empty squares. A mirror turns either colour. Dark blocks stop light.

    Workshop guide

    Amber begins at the left edge; blue begins below. Match each coloured beam to its outlined receiver on the border. Mirrors are reusable: lift or rotate one when your supply is full. The numbered grid buttons describe row and column. Arrow keys move selection; / and B place mirrors, Delete lifts, Z undoes. Native Tab/Enter controls also work. P is reserved for Study Desk.

    No timer or loss penalty. Undo and restart preserve records. Three small authored windows become more constrained; replay for fewer placements. Saves are local to this browser. The 150-action journal bound is for storage, not a goal.
