THREE PORTS, ONE SMALL HOLD

Brine Ledger

Carry what the next harbour needs.

On the quay

The shipping charters

Reinvest in your boat

Your first voyage

Buy two salt at Saltfen, keep two coins for passage, and sail to Weft Bay. Deliver the winter-catch charter there. Then inspect the other ports: each produces one cheap good and needs another.

Each item takes one hold space. Buying and selling do not advance time. Sailing costs two coins and advances the market cycle once; a trimmed sail reduces passage to one. The larger hold carries six items. Every port card shows its next-arrival buy prices; selling pays one coin less.

Each charter accepts two of its named cargo at its destination, pays the local sale price plus five coins, and is delivered once per ledger. Complete all three for a merchant seal, then choose repeatable merchant manifests. Each offers a fixed payment for a mixed cargo: small parcels fit the original hold; bulk freight requires the larger hold. Accept only one at a time, buy where cheap, and deliver at its named port. New offers rotate goods after a delivery; releasing a manifest keeps cargo but earns nothing. Compare the fixed reward against purchase and passage costs. Then keep trading or start again. Your best net worth survives a new ledger; coins, upgrades and cargo start fresh. Net worth uses current-port buy prices, so it is an estimate, not spendable cash.

If you cannot afford passage, sell cargo. With no cargo and insufficient coins, start a new ledger. Tab and Space operate every control. Progress saves after each action. No clock runs while you are away.
