One tooth at a time

Tap a dial to select it, then turn left or right. Each turn moves one of six positions. A dial shows its current number and its target moon mark.

Engaged neighbours turn opposite ways. Movement travels through every connected dial. The coupling buttons let you disconnect one link; a second choice reconnects the first. Use this to work on part of the machine without moving the rest.

Start by opening A–B, then turning A to its target. Reconnect when you want to move the chain together. Undo is unlimited within the current journal; there is no timer or failure penalty.

Keys 1–5 select dials; A/D turn; Z undoes. Win to unlock the next mechanism. Your selected dial, fewest turns and current journal save automatically. Labels on each dial show whether it turns with the selected dial or remains isolated. Clutch changes do not count as turns. Stuck? Show a next step suggests one move from your current position.
