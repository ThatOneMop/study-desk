Field guide & controls

Each of six surveys offers three finds: a local plant, a local animal and a mixed find. Keep two; leave one in the field. You can repeat a survey location. Arrange all twelve finds to finish. Reach the trail target to unlock the next trail. You may undo any action, including opening a packet; the same seed always offers the same finds.

Tap/click a card and a space, then Place. Keyboard: 1–3 selects a survey or packet card; arrows select an album space; Enter places. Native buttons and fields keep their normal keyboard behaviour. Once per album, Rearrange moves a find to an empty space or swaps two different finds. Select the source in the dropdown, then a destination and confirm. It also works on completed pages. Undo restores the rearrangement. No time limit. Pause with the toolbar.

Seeds vary packets, not the terrain. Records keep your highest score per trail across seeds. This is a compact collection game, with no purchases or paid packs.
