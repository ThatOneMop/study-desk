# Creekhold credits

Original Study Desk game. The code, maps, interface, illustration, and synthesized sound were written for this collection.

No art, audio, text, logos, or level geometry from other colony games is included. Public notes on how river-colony games tend to work are kept in `docs/creekhold-research.md` and are a mechanics reference only.

Saves use `study-desk-creekhold-v1` in local storage.
