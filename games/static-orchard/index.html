FIELD GUIDE

Make the swarm your circuit.

Hold the orchard to charge the selected fruit. Keep your pointer on a moving pest, then release. On keyboard, use the arrows to move the crosshair and hold Space. Press T to track the next pest automatically while you charge with Space; arrow movement or pointer aiming returns to manual aim. The dotted arc shows exactly which neighbors will link at the current charge. A quick spark hits one pest, a medium charge chains through three, and a full charge chains through five. Aim within the pest’s ring; arcs need nearby neighbors.

Select west, centre or east fruit with the buttons or 1 / 2 / 3. Fruit can reach 440 orchard pixels. A beetle’s shell absorbs one damage per hit. The queen’s violet shield blocks arcs while closed.

Defeated pests supply harvest energy. The ground pulse spends four energy to damage and push back pests within the selected fruit’s dotted 180-pixel circle, bypassing armor. It is an emergency tool for nearby pests, not the whole orchard. Save energy for a better harvest record, or spend it to protect the trees.

Clear three swarms to save a grove. Buy permanent tools between defenses. Records award seals only once; improving a record earns the difference. Replay with new windseeds for different entrances.

Focus loss pauses and cancels your charge. Reload restores your exact saved swarm, paused. Pause or leave the page to save immediately; the game also autosaves during play.
