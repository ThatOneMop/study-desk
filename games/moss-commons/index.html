The settlement handbook

Start from the central hall. Every new building or path must touch the existing settlement along an edge. Any building connects the settlement; paths are cheap ways to reach distant plots. Rocks cannot be built on. Dotted plots are fertile.

A house shelters 2 people. A well supplies 3 water to the connected settlement. A garden produces 1 food, plus 1 on fertile ground and plus 2 if beside a well. More neighbouring wells do not stack that bonus. An orchard costs 3 timber and produces 6 food when at least two edge-neighbouring plots are empty, buildable meadow (fertile meadow counts). With fewer than two, it produces only 1 food. Rocks, buildings, paths and the map edge do not count as open meadow. Fertility and wells do not boost orchards. Building on its open ground can reduce its yield immediately; undo restores it. All numbers are sustainable capacity, not resources waiting for a timer.

Neighbourhood challenge: finish a standard site to unlock this optional redesign. Each home must share an edge with a well and a food plot (garden or orchard), while total food and water still meet population demand. One neighbour can serve multiple homes. Paths and diagonal neighbours do not carry local service. Orchards still need open meadow to produce enough food. The forecast lists homes still missing a service. Challenge records are separate; starting either mode restarts only the current layout after confirmation, and retry keeps the current mode.

Convert building: select an occupied plot and its new use. Pay 1 timber for labour plus any increase in building cost; cheaper conversions still cost 1 and give no refund. The hall cannot change. Connections stay, but food yield, population and local services recalculate. A path can become a well, or a poorly placed garden an orchard. This can preserve later construction, while undo remains free. Inspect the cost and capacity forecast before committing.

Relocate: choose a building in the source selector, then select an empty destination plot. Moving costs 1 timber and keeps the building type. All occupied plots must still connect to the central hall after the move; you cannot move the hall. Food is recalculated at the new location, including fertility, wells and orchard space. The forecast shows the new totals before you commit. Undo restores both location and timber. Finish your redesign before completing the site.

Win with at least 3 houses, food and water each at least equal to population. Extra houses create extra demand. Select a plot and a building, inspect the forecast, then build. Undo is unlimited and preserves earned records; retry starts this site over. Try finishing with more timber left. Keyboard: Tab into the chart, arrows select plots, then Tab to the action controls. Enter/Space activates buttons; selectors retain normal keys.
