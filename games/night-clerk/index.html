The night desk handbook

Select a guest and read their arrival note. Ask about their stay reveals both exact needs; questions are free. Choose one of six rooms, then Hand over key. One guest per room.

Rooms beside each other, or directly above/below each other, are neighbours. Diagonals are not. Guests marked with a music note make noise.

A quiet sleeper needs a quiet room with no noisy neighbour. After two completed shifts, felt screens can make any room quiet and block incoming guest noise. They do not change friendship, avoidance, floor or window requirements.

Ring for lights-out when everyone has a room. Complaints explain what to fix; after three unsettled calls you must restart. Earlier records remain safe.

All actions work with Tab and Enter or touch. There is no time limit. Your current shift and records save automatically.
