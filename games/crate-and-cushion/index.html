Dispatch book

Original code and CSS art · Three compact packing jobs · Development prototype

Pack it snug. Pack it safe.

Fit all four parcels without overlaps or crossing the dark wooden braces. Each parcel rotates by a quarter turn. Selecting an already packed parcel lets you move it directly, or lift it out first.

The telescope (T) needs at least two shared square edges with the foam (F). The heavy toolbox (H) must not touch the telescope along an edge. Blankets (B) can act as a spacer. Corners do not count as touching.

The preview shows fit before you place. Packing is reversible: undo, lift and repack freely. Seal the crate when all three checks pass to unlock the next job. Replay to minimise placements and lifts; each changes your move count. No timer or failure penalty.

Settings and placements save automatically. Only this game's save is affected by New dispatch book.
