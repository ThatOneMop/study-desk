THE LITTLE RAILWAY AT DAYBREAK

Switchyard Post

Deliver the red and blue mail wagons to their matching platforms. Uncouple and park the locomotive at its gold home marker. No clock. Undo the last 200 actions.

How to shunt

Drive into an uncoupled wagon to push it one space along straight track. To pull around a corner, stop beside a wagon, couple it, then drive into empty track: the wagon follows into your previous space. You can pull only one wagon. Uncouple before pushing or delivering. A wagon on its platform may be moved again if it blocks your route.

Tap adjacent track or use arrow buttons. On the yard: arrows drive, R/B couple or release, Z undoes. Tab/Enter works on every button. Completing yards unlocks the next and a fragile-mail contract for that yard. Fragile red mail must be pulled, never pushed. Platform exchange sends it to a sheltered platform instead of the dead end. Earn three fragile badges and separate best routes; switch back to standard at any time. Coupling also counts as an action.

Original code and railway illustration. Three small authored yards.
