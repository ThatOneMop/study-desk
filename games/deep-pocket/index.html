    A small decision.

    First time at the lake?

    Pick a bank and depth. The field guide predicts which fish you will hook. Open the habitat chart to compare all nine bank/depth choices, survey targets, the trip's specific stewardship task and the next two tides at your selected water. Each cast advances the tide; there are eight casts per field trip.

    During a catch, choose Reel, Brace or Give line. The fish's next pull adds to your action's tension change. Reach 100 distance before beat 14, keeping tension strictly between 0 and 100. Slack, a snapped line or running out of beats loses the fish. Three escapes end the trip. There is no real-time clock.

    Land a fish, then keep it for shells or release it for nursery goals. A species released for field work cannot later be sold in the same trip, so each keep/release choice matters. The stall takes only one of each species, and nursery goals need different released species. Either records its species. Fulfil the shell, nursery, survey and named keep/release field task, then return. Earn a medal for finishing, another for no escapes, and another for finishing within the displayed cast target. Only better replays award more medals.

    Medals buy equipment between trips. New equipment applies on the next trip or retry. Your journal and progress save in this browser when storage is available. Retry resets only the current trip. Undo steps back through this trip’s fishing actions, including casts, escapes, catch decisions, field notes and medal rewards. Bank/depth and chart choices neither add to nor erase that history; undo restores the location used by the earlier fishing action. Upgrades, retry and trip navigation clear undo. Reload preserves the trip but starts fresh undo history.

    Keyboard: Tab and Enter/Space for every control; R / B / E during a catch; Z undoes. Exact action previews include the next fish pull.
