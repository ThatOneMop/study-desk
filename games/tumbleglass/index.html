A SMALL STUDY IN GRAVITY

Tumbleglass

Tilt the whole tray. Nest each glass bead in its matching numbered socket.

At the glassmaker’s bench

All loose beads slide as far as they can. The bead nearest the tilted edge moves first. Walls and other beads stop them. A bead locks immediately when it passes over its own numbered socket. Locked beads never move — plan which one to settle first. Other sockets do not stop it. Once per attempt, you may clamp one loose bead in place for a single tilt. Choose it before tilting; the other beads still slide. Save the clamp for a tricky turn, or solve without it. A tilt that moves nothing does not spend the clamp. Undo restores it; retry gives it back.

Use the arrow keys on the page or tray, or the four tilt buttons. The small number on a bead matches its socket, so colour is optional. Undo is unlimited; retry keeps your best records. Solve a tray to unlock the next, then try fewer tilts. No clock and no lives. Optional Check tray searches all legal tilts and the unused clamp. It shows one move on a shortest remaining route, or explains that undo is needed even if beads can still move. It never changes your layout, selected clamp or save. Guidance clears after a successful tilt, undo or restart.
