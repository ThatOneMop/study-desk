Welcome, wanderer

One person's lost thing…

…is your next little discovery. Wander the coast with your metal detector. The signal gets stronger as you approach buried treasure.

Move: WASD, arrows, or click the ground.
Pinpoint: hold SPACE to stand still and follow the arrow.
Read the screen: high target IDs ring a high tone and are usually treasure. Low IDs are usually junk; the junk filter [Q] mutes them.
Dig: E or the Dig button, once per shovel stroke. Deeper targets need more strokes.
Grow: Sell finds, upgrade your gear and unlock new places.
Your goal: Find all 15 treasures, including the lighthouse key.

No timer. No energy bar. Just one more find.
