A QUIET SERVICE

Listen. Blend. Remember.

Each ghost describes a favourite tea. Add up to three scoops; mix leaves with herbs or honey. Choose water temperature, steeping method and water amount, then Brew & pour and Offer the cup.

Every ingredient changes floral aroma, body, sweetness and roasted warmth. Longer or hotter steeps extract more, but can taste harsh. More water softens the cup. Visitors tell you which flavours need more or less, and whether the temperature feels right.

A score of 80 or more restores a memory and invites the next visitor. You have three cups per visit; a disappointed visitor can always be invited back. Restart preserves memories, tools and tasted recipes.

Memories earn two seals once. Spend them on a silver strainer, recipe cards or a half-measure spoon. The spoon lets you divide the same three-scoop capacity into smaller portions for finer flavour ratios. Choose whole or half scoops above the ingredients; the choice saves with your visit. New ingredients arrive after two, three and four memories. Replaying a remembered visitor can improve the record, but does not farm seals.

The live flavour preview shows what will reach the cup before you brew. It compares your blend with the last tasting for this visitor. After a tasting, Refine this blend keeps its ingredients and brewing settings for your next attempt, while Start a fresh blend empties the pot. Neither refunds a used attempt. Undo reverses your last recipe adjustment without spending a cup; the preview never spends an attempt.

All controls work with touch, mouse or keyboard Tab and Enter/Space. Your visit saves after every decision. There is no clock to race.
