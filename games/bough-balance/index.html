A little brass weight

An optional weight for the opening mobile. Clip it to a beam, slide it, or take it off. It weighs one unit. Try a different arrangement of birds; the weight can make up a small imbalance. Each change costs one action. Finish with the weight hanging to earn a separate brass-arrangement record; your usual best record stays.

How balance works

Every beam weighs one unit, centred at its pivot. For each hanging object, multiply its weight by its signed arm distance: left is negative, right positive. A beam balances when these pulls add to zero. A beam’s own centred weight creates no pull on itself, but counts in the weight felt by its parent. The diagram keeps beams level for reading; the meters show their actual imbalance.

Every bird has a letter and weight. Choose it and a numbered hook, then inspect the exact forecast before hanging it. A bird already on another hook swaps with the selected hook’s occupant. A bird from the tray displaces the current occupant back to the tray. Return to tray removes just the selected hook’s bird. Nested beams are fixed and cannot be moved. On the opening mobile, the optional brass weight has its own four marked positions; it can share an arm with a bird or suspension cord. It never takes a bird hook. Moving it removes its old effect before adding the new one. Undo restores both the birds and brass weight.

There is no clock or failure penalty. Undo and restart are always available, including after success; records survive. All hooks must be filled and every beam balanced to finish. Replay for fewer actions. The 200-action save limit is a storage bound, not a target: undo or restart if reached.

Tab/Enter/Space operate all controls. On the page or bird/hook buttons: 1–6 selects a bird, arrows select a hook, H hangs, R returns a bird to the tray, Z undoes. Other controls keep normal keys. Pause stops input.
