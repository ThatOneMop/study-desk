A LITTLE TUG, A LONG WAY HOME

Reed Convoy

Gather the reeds. Leave room for your tail.

How to tow

The red tug leads; each boat follows the space ahead of it. Collect a crate by steering onto it: a cargo boat joins the tail. Return the tug to HOME and unload any carried crates to shorten the convoy. Deliver all three.

You cannot enter rocks or your own boats, except the tail space when it moves away. Collecting a crate keeps the tail in place. Plan wide loops and bank a partial load if the long convoy blocks your route. No timer or lives; blocked moves cost nothing. Restart preserves completed route records.

Arrow keys or touch steer. U unloads; Tab and Space/Enter work on buttons. Every movement saves.
