WELCOME, SMALL POSTAL SOCIETY

A world in a letter.

Collect every postage stamp and reach the lighthouse with the chart completely flat. Fold a shore over its opposite half, cross to an island that now touches yours, then unfold to travel somewhere new.

Folding mirrors one half of the sheet across its middle. Your traveller stays on the same physical island as it moves. You can walk to an island one square away or cross between stacked islands. Diagonals do not connect. The crossing list makes every stacked island accessible.

Only one axis may be folded at a time. Open the current crease before folding a different shore. Every fold and unfold spends one crease; walking spends none. Thread spools repair two creases, once each. If your last crease leaves no way home and no reachable spool, rewind.

Landing on wet ink costs one heart, once per island. The stain then dries. Three lost hearts end the journey. There is no clock; take your time. Seals reward clean, economical routes.

Keyboard: A/D fold left/right, W/S fold top/bottom, X unfold. Number keys 1–9 preview listed crossings. Enter confirms a preview; Escape cancels. Z rewinds. Tab and Enter work on buttons. Repeated keys, modifiers and shortcuts inside dialogs are ignored. P belongs to Study Desk.

Planning: choose a fold or crossing to see the exact future chart, supplies, damage and touching islands. Nothing is spent until Make this move. The preview map is read-only; use Cancel to return to the current map. A red preview warns when the move ends your journey.

Your current chart and best seals save in this browser. Five charts unlock in order. Fresh sheet asks before restarting; rewind is free.

With one bridge active at a time, stitch a bridge to an island exactly two squares away on the current folded map. Preview and confirm to spend two creases and cross it. The thread joins those same islands even after unfolding, and can be crossed both ways for free. Ink, stamps and spools still work normally. While the journey is active, unpick a bridge from either endpoint to recover one crease and free the thread for another crossing. This removes its connection, so preview the remaining paths first. Rebuilding costs two creases: there is no free thread loop. Every stitch counts as two extra folds toward the clean-route seal, even after unpicking. Rewind restores the previous bridge and budget.
