    Tonight's exact forecast

    From empty bed to supper

    Each day gives three work points. Select a bed, then sow, pick, water or compost. Each costs one point. Finish the workday to grow crops overnight. Take as long as you like to plan: nothing moves on a clock. You have eight workdays; the final evening closes the season without another growth step.

    Beans: two growing nights, no soil cost, two portions. Picking restores one soil to that bed and gives one compost. Carrots: one growing night, cost one soil, two portions. Squash: three growing nights, cost two soil, five portions. Replanting carrots or squash immediately after the same crop in that bed yields one fewer portion. Beans break this repeat penalty.

    Green manure: after at least one growing night, you can turn a bean plant into the soil instead of harvesting food. This costs one work point, restores two soil to its bed (maximum two), gives one compost bag and breaks the repeat-crop penalty. It gives no bean portions and removes the plant’s shade. You can also do this to ripe beans if you prefer the extra soil to food.

    Water and companions: a rainy night first adds one water to every bed (maximum two). A growing crop then needs one water and uses it. A squash beside a standing bean plant keeps its water while growing; it still needs some water to begin with. All neighbours mean up/down/left/right. Picking the beans removes that shade. Mature crops stop drinking and never spoil.

    Watering fills the selected bed and its neighbours to two water. Compost adds one soil to a bed (maximum two), using a compost bag. You start with two bags. Soil is spent when sowing; a crop cannot be removed except by picking it or undoing its planting.

    Fill all three basket requests to finish and unlock another garden. Records count work points left, including future days: efficient routes score higher. A missed basket can be retried; undo also works after a season ends. Retry keeps records. Forecasts are free, and you can inspect the whole weather sequence.

    Keyboard: Tab / Enter / Space for every control. On the page or beds, arrows select a bed, 1–3 choose seeds, S sows, H picks, W waters, C composts, G turns beans into soil and Z undoes. Shortcuts never override other controls.
