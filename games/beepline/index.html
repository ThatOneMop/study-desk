THE LITTLE FLIGHT COMPUTER

Beepline

Write a repeating flight program. Visit every flower, then return to the hive. Fewer commands make a tidier dance.

Your loop

Starts facing east →. Commands repeat from the beginning. Editing rewinds the bee; your records stay.

Flight handbook

F — Forward: fly one square; hitting a hedge ends the flight. G — Glide: fly until the square before a hedge, collecting flowers along the way. L / R: turn left or right without moving.

Use up to six commands. All turns are relative to the bee’s current direction. Step follows a glide one square at a time so you can inspect its route. The highlighted command is the next one to execute, or the glide currently in progress.

Visit each pink flower and return home to unlock the next garden. Rewind and edit as often as you like. A repeating state means this exact program cannot finish. There is no clock. Space steps when focus is on the board or page; form controls use their normal keys. Run flight animates the same steps and can be stopped.
