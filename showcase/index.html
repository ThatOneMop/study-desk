How this was built

Bellows Gate is an original one-level defence, drawn for this page. It borrows the mood of a forge yard from the Study Desk collection and none of its saves, names, or catalogue entries.

An AI coding assistant drafted the rules and the paper-forge drawing, then checked them: a tiny script proves an empty yard loses and a planned yard can hold, and a headless browser walked the start, a win, a loss, and the phone layout.
