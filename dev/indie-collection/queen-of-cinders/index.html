Field notes · how to play

1. Dig from the queen's connected tunnels to ● food, ≈ water and ◆ resin. Each dig costs one food. Planning never advances time.

2. Assign six ants using + / −. Long routes need more carrier work per supply. Each shift feeds the queen one food. Nurses hatch one brood for 3 food, 1 resin and 3 nursery work. Each brood adds an available worker.

3. Send shifts yourself; there is no real-time clock. Fire closes exposed tunnels at the end of a shift. Dampen critical tunnels for 2 water, even after burning, to keep routes connected. Supply patches are finite.

Rescue the target brood before the final shift. Earn medals for rescue, no queen damage, and finishing at least four shifts early. Spend medals between chapters. Your best-only scores prevent replay farming.

Check the next-shift forecast before committing. Supply trails show carrier work, stored work, next deliveries and whether fire cuts a route after those deliveries. Trace highlights only soil already excavated, including burned paths; it does not reveal a solution through undug ground. Inspect (3) spends nothing and preserves undo. Undo (Z) restores your last dig, allocation, dampening or entire shift, even a final rescue reward. Upgrades, changing chapter and retry clear undo. Reload keeps your colony but not undo history.

Keyboard: Tab / Enter or Space for buttons, arrows across earth, 1 dig, 2 dampen, 3 inspect. On very narrow screens, scroll the earth sideways; every cell stays finger-sized. Progress saves after each action. This is a short five-chapter prototype, not a 20-hour game.
