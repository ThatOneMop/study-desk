How the water works

Inspect a numbered tile. Cut it to height 0 to make a channel, raise it to height 4 to make a bank, or restore its original grade. Removed earth stays in your barrow; filling needs that same earth. Fixed roofs, cisterns, homes and gardens cannot be dug. Cutting/filling changes pressure without creating or deleting water. It does not flow until you choose Flow or Rain.

Water seeks the lowest neighboring surface: head = height × 4 + water depth. Each step sends at most two drops toward that neighbor, never more than half the head difference. Closed cisterns receive water but hold all outflow. Open them to release storage. One action resolves up to 40 tiny flow steps, stopping when settled; there is no wall-clock timer. Thin pools can remain in flat channels. Regrading a wet tile lifts its water surface and can redirect trapped rain.

Gardens drink one drop per flow step at depth 1–4 until restored. Deeply flooded gardens cannot drink until depth falls. A home at depth 4+ takes one damage per flow step; three damage floods the catchment. Depth above 16 spills out of the model. Finish all three gardens before flooding; earlier districts and best low-rain records are saved. Four short handmade catchments, no content-hours claim.

Touch/click a tile, then use the tool buttons. The map can scroll horizontally on small screens. Keyboard arrows move inspection, Home/End choose the first/last tile; Enter/Space inspect. While focused on a tile: 1 cut, 2 bank, 3 original grade, 4 cistern, R rain, F flow, Z undo. Arrows on tiles show the next tiny flow step after garden drinking. Forecasts apply the actual simulation without changing your catchment. Tool forecasts distinguish the immediate earthwork from what would happen if you then choose Flow. Undo restores terrain, water, growth, flooding and earned district/journal records; inspection does not fill history. Undo history is session-only and clears on reload, replan or district change. Saves retain terrain, stored water, growth, cisterns, selection and village records. Restart/closing are confirmed.
