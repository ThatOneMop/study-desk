THE LAST DELIVERY CLOSES THE STALL

The six market awnings

A little guide to barter

Each visitor has two bundles but will make only one accepted trade this market. Inspect their values: a timber might be worth 3 to Fern, but only 1 as a stall reserve. Put any owned goods on the offer tray with + / −, choose a bundle and barter. Their valuation must meet the ask; excess valuation is overpayment. The interface blocks under-value offers; earlier declined offers in a saved market still count toward three declines closing the stall. A full shelf blocks a trade without using patience.

Craft tea, balm, vessels and lamps from stock. Deliver community orders in any order, but remember delivered goods cannot be offered later. Your final order closes the stall immediately. Extra seals reward fair trades and a useful reserve of stock. Six short handmade markets have different starting stock, shelf space and order demands; no wall-clock timer.

Draft offers and selected visitors are saved as you adjust them. Recipes/deliveries/committed trades clear the tray to prevent stale offers. Enter/Space activate native controls; arrow keys move visitor focus. Z undoes up to 100 committed trades, crafts, deliveries or failed closes in this session, restoring draft offers, inventory and banked records. Draft inspection does not fill undo history; repacking, changing markets and reloading clear it. Each game uses its own browser save. These prices are a game, not a real-world pricing model.

Original code-native woodland stall, visitor seals and goods. No third-party game or external assets. Progress saves in this browser.
