Pocket Expedition

There is always room for one more find. Is there enough food to bring it home?

Before you leave camp

Choose up to two tools and 4–9 rations. Tools use one pack unit each; food and finds share the remaining space. Capacity is 12. A load above 10 makes every trail cost one extra ration. Travelling consumes food and frees pack space.

Choose a linked site, then Trek. Rope protects ridges, boots protect marshes and the lamp protects tunnels: the matching trail costs one ration, not two, with no terrain injury risk. Unprotected terrain can injure you; weather changes its risk. Recovery costs one ration and has its own displayed injury chance; the lamp lowers that chance. You still recover the find if injured, unless a third injury ends the expedition.

Return to camp carries the pack along the lowest-ration route; equal-cost routes favour lower injury risk. You can also trek home manually. Finds are only banked at camp. Bring back any copy of the region's relic to unlock the next survey; clean and high-value returns earn extra seals. Early sample-only retreats still add to the journal. Running out of food away from camp or taking three injuries loses this run's finds, not earlier records.

Click map sites to inspect. Arrow keys/Home/End move map focus; Enter/Space inspects without travelling. Weather advances after every trek and recovery, not inspection or dropping cargo. Z undoes up to 100 logical actions in this session, including injuries, dropped cargo, failed returns and banked finds. Undo restores the same random state, never rerolls it. Departure, repacking, survey changes and page reload clear undo history. Nothing runs by wall clock. Autosave follows actions; retry starts a different seeded run, not a guaranteed roll. Six short development-only expeditions; fictional travel rules, not outdoor advice.
