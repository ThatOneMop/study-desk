How the nursery works

Preview both offspring, then cross and keep one new cultivar. A planted seedling can become a parent. Six pots are available; prune a tree to make room. Pruning keeps its discovery in your album, but removes that parent from the nursery. At least two trees stay. You can return seedlings and try another crossover.

Match every requested trait and deliver. Each new order starts with four parent trees; discoveries and best crossing counts persist. Three fixed requests form this initial prototype. There is no timer. Tab and Space operate every button; touch works directly. Every action saves.
