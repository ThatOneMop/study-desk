Camera notes

Make an 80-point photograph of each animal. Nine frames per outing; your best prints survive a new outing. Select a subject and frame its position. A centered subject scores better than one at the edge; subjects outside the three-position viewfinder are missed.

Exposure = light + aperture − shutter; aim for 2. Narrow/slow are 0, middle is 1, wide/fast are 2. Faster shutters freeze motion but darken the frame. Focus at the subject's shown distance. The preview explains the current quality before you spend film.

Animals move only when you watch or take a photograph. Tab and Space operate every button; touch works directly. Nothing runs on a clock. Every action saves. This is a compact prototype, not a photography tutorial or a long campaign.
