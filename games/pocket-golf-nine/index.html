A ROUND AT YOUR OWN PACE

A good day for nine.

Pull back from the ball, then release to shoot in the opposite direction. Aim for the cup. Banks, sand, water and a little wind make the journey interesting.

The putting green is ready.

    Follow the dotted line

    Drag back from the ball to choose direction and power. Release to play; release near the ball to cancel. Or use ← → to aim, ↑ ↓ for power and Space to shoot. Buttons and the power slider work with touch or keyboard.

    A few local rules

    Sand slows the ball. Water returns it to the previous shot and adds one penalty stroke. Boards are useful for bank shots; round bumpers have a lively bounce. The cup catches slower balls. Finish with fewer strokes for a better record. Progress and records save in this browser.
