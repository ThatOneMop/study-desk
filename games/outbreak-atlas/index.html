    Infection crosses the water.

    Choose a story, Drift or Root, and how hard cure pressure pushes back. You never play the cure. Land routes are already open. Every island can be reached by sea or by sky.

    How infection spreads

    Each month infection grows where it already lives, then spreads along open routes. Land routes are always open. Harbor threads open every sea route. Sky threads open every sky route. Buying either one can reach every island, so an island is never a dead end.

    Drift spreads faster and raises awareness quickly. Root spreads slower and stays quiet. Awareness feeds cure pressure. If cure pressure fills, the story ends. You cannot play as the cure.

    A foothold and an established infection each give points. Spend them on Tide, Veil, or Hold. You cannot buy every upgrade. The outlook guesses the next month; it is not a promise.

    Quiet Circuit wants infection established in 8 lands while awareness is still half or lower. Twin Lights wants Lantern Isles and Farwhistle. Hearthhold wants Hearthlow heavily infected for 8 months. World Spread and Calm Atlas want all 14 lands.
