# Study Desk curation — 29 October 2026

The owner requested fewer weak games and stronger graphics, fun and progression. The collection now has 46 entries (45 bundled, one external). Twenty entries were removed from the initial 65, and the concurrently published Lane Eight swimming game is preserved. This is an editorial assessment of the implemented loops, not a player-rating result or a promise that every retained game has been playtested to completion.

## Removed from the collection

The ten Nightshift mini-campaigns and ten Afterlight mini-campaigns are retired from the catalogue, filters, recommendations and random picks. Their shared engines implement short repeated tasks with less build and route depth than the retained standalone games. Their files, old links, preferences and browser saves are preserved. `retired-catalog-2026-10-29.json` records every retired entry, so the change can be reversed.

## Six games improved

| Game | Graphics | Fun and progression |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Autumn Drift | Ambient motes and evening fireflies; a visible next-reward goal; illustrated cover | Bare hands pick individually; rake rank 5 unlocks sweeping. Five upgrade ranks per track open in each area. Picking now contributes to streak bonuses. One-third and two-thirds clearance pay garden rewards, with the existing full-clear bonus retained. |
| Little Lake | Water lighting and moving reflections; more distinct fish silhouettes; illustrated lure and contract panels; cover | Three free lures target different species with different bite delays. Three persistent contracts reward diversity, perfect catches and a full fishing trip. Rewards pay once; a completed trip opens the next. |
| Underleaf | Work-priority styling and a colony cover | Four work priorities trade food-trip speed against hatching speed or chamber cost. A 15-second commitment makes priorities meaningful. Old colonies default to balanced work. |
| After Hours | Deck-composition bars, a visual card-removal workshop and cover | Remove one card per shop to shape future draws. Cost scales by ante; a minimum deck size and empty-deck guards prevent invalid play. Existing 52-card saves remain valid. |
| Ratchet Run | Forest, canyon and snowfield scenery plus a cover | Three authored routes extend the campaign from five to eight. Course unlocks, best ghosts and old recordings survive migration. Optional time targets and immediate post-result retries support repeat attempts. |
| Ashwake | Relic sigils, role/rank labels and cover | Two relic redraws per watch let players redirect a build. Redraws persist across reloads. Pausing at a relic choice now preserves the offered cards on reload. |

The six updated games appear on the Freshly improved shelf. Covers are labelled as illustrations, not gameplay screenshots. Gummy Bay and the other retained games are not claimed as new gameplay overhauls in this change.

## Verification

- New gameplay suite covers once-only rewards, lure pools, invalid/legacy state, deck removal, limited redraws, paused-level recovery, priority tradeoffs and cooldowns, tool gates and milestones.
- All eight bike routes are completed through the actual fixed-step engine; replays and five-route save migration are checked.
- Existing save/recovery, controls, catalogue, storefront and asset checks are run for the affected code.
- Autumn Drift was opened and a leaf collected in the browser preview. During later checks the shared preview switched to another project. Full browser and mobile visual verification for the other changes remains incomplete.
- Cover SVGs were rendered and visually inspected separately.

GitHub Pages deployment status is reported separately after publication. A passing source test does not establish that the live website has updated.
