How the colony grows

Fit one specialization per excavated chamber for four soil. Pantry frees foragers, bastion frees guards and nursery frees nurses. Spending soil on fittings delays the next chamber; choose what your colony needs. Fittings last until restart. Repeat jobs restores yesterday’s assignment, then adjust for the forecast.

Assign each ant one job using + and −. Foragers gather food; each pair of workers eats one food per day. Digging yields one soil per worker. Spend six soil on a chamber to expand storage and brood size.

Lay brood, then assign one nurse for each pair of eggs on two days to hatch workers. Growing the colony increases upkeep. Guards absorb the forecast's threat; spare guards repair one nest health. Food shortages and unguarded threats damage the nest.

Establish a daughter colony with at least 10 workers, 2 chambers, 22 food and 4 health. There is no timer. Review the next three forecasts before advancing. Every action saves. Touch, or Tab and Space/Enter, operate all controls. Retry keeps completed records.
