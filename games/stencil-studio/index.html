Ink stays on paper

Match the commission exactly within five operations (prints or ink lifts). Sea, rose and sun inks combine wherever they overlap. Lift selected ink removes only that colour under the stencil, keeping other colours and later layers. It uses one operation; an empty lift costs nothing. Undo always lets you try again.

Choose a stencil and ink, rotate it, then tap a bed square or use arrow keys to position it. The outlined cells preview the resulting mixed colours. The forecast warns about extra ink and shows how many squares will still differ. Your stencil, rotation, ink and position are saved even before printing. Press Print or Space to stamp. In lift mode, inspect the preview then press Lift selected ink. Lift mode resets to print on reload; committed lifts are saved.

Use “Compare planned result on the bed” to see exactly which inks each planned square needs added (+) or lifted (−); a tick means it matches. This includes your stencil preview and does not spend an operation. Use “show only the selected ink” to understand a colour layer. A stencil spilling off the paper cannot print. Win to unlock the next commission; replay to use fewer prints. There is no timer.
