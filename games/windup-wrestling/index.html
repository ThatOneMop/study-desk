The workshop

Win the edge

Push your rival beyond either striped edge. Read their next move before you act. You act first; the rival follows.

Wind stores spring up to your kit’s capacity. Shove spends all spring to push an adjacent rival that many spaces. Brace absorbs your kit’s guard strength.

Slip spends your kit’s slip cost. If an adjacent rival grapples, swap sides and send them past you using their own spring. If they do something else, your spring is wasted.

Keys 1–6 choose moves. Z undoes. No clock runs. Try different routes for a shorter winning bout.

The first bell

You are the mint toy; your rival is coral. Each action advances one rival move. Spaces −3 and +3 are the edges; beyond them is a ring out.

Try winding while the rival is far away. Once adjacent, shove them towards the edge. A rival bracing can absorb a weak shove. When a strong grapple is announced, slip can turn their momentum against them.

Every move is reversible. Winning unlocks the next rival and records your fewest turns. Your first bell unlocks two mechanical kits with different spring, guard and slip costs. Kit changes restart the bout after confirmation. Standard bells open rivals; workshop medals track alternate solutions.
