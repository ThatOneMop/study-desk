A WORKSHOP BY THE WOODS

Grainwright

Find the shape inside the timber.

Furniture commissions

Your tool chest

At the workbench

Select a numbered station. The pale line is the requested height; the wood above it must go. A stroke removes one layer from every station covered by your plane, starting at your selection and moving left or right.

Follow each arrow in the wood. Cutting against the grain leaves rough fibres; sand them away before delivery. Cutting below the pale line spoils the board. Replacing a board keeps your coins and tools.

Deliveries pay 4 coins, plus 2 for finishing within the single-station stroke allowance. Buy wider planes to shape several stations in a stroke; fine abrasive removes two roughness marks at once. Later boards mix grain directions.

Furniture commissions unlock with the triple plane after two deliveries. Earn either seal to open the next design. Clean seals allow no tear-out, even if sanded away. Efficiency seals require delivery within a stroke budget; tear-out is allowed if repaired. A scraper cuts across any grain without tear-out, but costs two strokes. Each new seal pays 5 coins once; retrying keeps all seals. Selecting a commission replaces the current board after confirmation.

Keyboard: Tab to a station and press Space. Tab through controls; Enter or Space activates a tool. Nothing happens while you are away.
