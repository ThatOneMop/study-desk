Burrow Express

Little trains. Shared tunnels. Every parcel matters.

How to run the railway

1. Select a train. Starting from its lettered depot, tap neighbouring squares to draw a route to its matching flag. Tap an earlier route square to trim. Solid rock is blocked; wet tunnel edges cost 2 material, dry edges 1. Shared edges are paid once.

2. Dispatch, then advance one beat at a time. Hold/release individual trains. Two trains entering the same square, swapping squares or entering a held train cause a collision. Following into a vacated square is safe. Delivered trains leave the track.

Keyboard: arrow keys move map focus; Enter/Space selects a square. No clock runs. Progress saves after actions. Six short original yards; prototype, not selected for publication.
