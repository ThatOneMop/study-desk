    How sheep move

    Quiet paws lets the dog walk without disturbing any sheep. Each quiet move still counts as one step; toggle it off to nudge again. Whistles work normally in either mode.

    Walking nudges sheep within 2 grid steps one cell farther from you. Whistling draws sheep 3–4 grid steps away one cell closer; nearby sheep stay still.

    Choose a name in Whistle to for a single-sheep call. Only that sheep approaches; every other sheep waits, including one blocking its path. The same 3–4-step range applies. Sheltered sheep cannot be called. A call with no movement costs no step. Changing the target clears the old preview; choose Whistle again before confirming.

    Sheep cannot cross rocks, your dog or another sheep. If two choose the same cell, both wait. Every sheep follows its own fixed compass preference; the preview shows its exact decision. The golden gate safely shelters each arrival. Dogs stay outside it.

    Arrow keys / WASD choose a direction, W means north. Space chooses whistle. Enter confirms. Z undoes. Buttons also work with standard Tab and Enter. Tap beside the dog to choose that direction.
