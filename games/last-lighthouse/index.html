Guide them home

The gold boat is waiting for your light. Tap relays to cycle straight · / · \. The glowing line previews the beam for free.

Signal when the beam reaches the boat. A missed signal costs one hull. Cranking buys power at the cost of one hull. Three damage sends the convoy back.

Keys 1–6 rotate relays; Space signals; Z undoes. No clock runs. Try to shelter every boat with two clean signals.

One more light

Optional: aim at the diamond lifeboat and spend two power to rescue it before your last convoy delivery. Finish the harbour to earn its pennant. Spare power can fund rescue or absorb a mistake; cranking costs hull. R switches targets for free.

Your seventh relay

One portable prism can change the beam route. Columns count left to right and rows top to bottom, from 1 to 7. Choose Plan prism, then tap clear water, or use the column and row selectors. A dashed turquoise beam shows the proposed route without spending power. Turn preview to try another angle, then Place prism to commit for one power. Cancel keeps the original route. Moving costs another power; rotation is free (7). It cannot cover a relay, rock, lighthouse or boat berth. Its previous position becomes water. This competes with rescue power; undo restores it.
