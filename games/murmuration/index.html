Split into two wings to go around central reed islands and collect food on both sides. Gather before narrow channels. Golden rings are food; dark hatched reeds turn birds back.

Keep six birds and collect the route’s food goal to unlock the next roost. Every route has fourteen feeding spots. Replay for three feathers. The dotted flight paths show the next two seconds at your current wind and formation. An amber warning means some birds will turn back unless you change course. Pause to plan without spending flight time. Pause whenever you need; reloading resumes paused at the exact saved position.

In clear air between reeds, share two collected food to call back up to two turned-back birds. You have two calls per migration. Food spent on calls no longer counts toward the roost goal.
