WELCOME, LIGHTKEEPER

Leave a trail. Bring them home.

You sail one square per turn. Light travels through water around rocks: docks light two steps, reed refuges one, floating lanterns three. A thrown lantern has six turns left after the throw.

Throw two cardinal spaces from your boat through clear water. You carry a limited oil supply, and only two lanterns can float at once. Reclaim the oldest adjacent lantern (R) to recover one oil. A refuge (R on the chart) grants one fresh oil once.

Moths move one water step toward the nearest lantern after every valid action. Ties favour the oldest light and north/east/south/west movement. They eat lanterns they reach. If any moth ends on or beside your boat, you lose one hull. Three hits end the attempt.

Collect every golden firefly jar and reach the striped dock. Earn one medal for delivery, two for no damage, three for a clean crossing within both the turn and lantern-throw targets. Records survive restart; undo is free. A 180-action safety limit ends very long attempts.

Keys: WASD/arrows row; Shift + direction throws; R reclaims; Space holds; Z undoes. Tap controls work too. P is reserved for Study Desk. Nothing advances while you read or plan.
