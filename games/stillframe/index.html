Camera notes

Each animal has three photo briefs: a portrait, its habitat and a movement study. Read the brief, watch the pose, and earn it with quality of at least 80. Accepted briefs and best scores survive a new outing. Nine film frames give you a perfect-set challenge; mistakes can be retried with fresh film without losing accepted work.

Watch a moment costs no film. Each animal cycles through Resting → Travelling → Travelling → Revealed → Resting. Nothing runs on a clock. Adjust settings freely, then choose your moment. Frame buttons slide the viewfinder: put an animal one position left or right of center for a third. For movement, leave room in front of its travel direction.

Exposure = light + aperture − shutter; aim for 2. Narrow/slow are 0, middle is 1, wide/fast are 2. Match focus to the shown distance. Hold still for resting portraits; Follow subject keeps travelling animals sharp at Medium shutter but blurs still subjects. This is a game abstraction, not a photography tutorial.

The LIVE view advances after every shot. Your contact sheet keeps the captured pose, composition and score. Completing all briefs unlocks alternate portrait and habitat compositions. Tab and Space operate every button; touch works directly. Every action saves.
